# CVAH Discovery Questionnaire (Phase 1)

Purpose: capture the hospital-specific facts the University needs to localize. The University is fully usable while these are being gathered; answers refine — never gate — the build. Complete with the practice manager and medical director. Estimated time: 60–90 minutes.

## A. Hospital workflow
1. Days/hours of operation; surgery days; dental days.
2. Appointment length and typical daily appointment count.
3. Walk-in/emergency policy.
4. Which practice-management software (PIMS) is used? (affects documentation lessons) `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED]`
5. Where are treatments performed (treatment area layout, runs, wards, isolation)?

## B. Staffing
6. Current headcount: DVMs, CVTs, assistants, CSRs.
7. Who will serve as: Program Administrator? Medical Reviewer (DVM)? Assessors (name each; CVT or DVM strongly preferred for technical sign-offs)?
8. Protected training time available per trainee per shift (target ≥45 min/day in Weeks 0–4).
9. Current assistants' experience levels (for challenge-out placement testing).

## C. Equipment (feeds 19_Equipment_Registry)
10. In-house lab: exact analyzer models + software versions (VetLab Station? ProCyte Dx/One? Catalyst One/Dx? SediVue? SNAP Pro? Imagyst?).
11. Imaging: radiography system (DR/CR, generator, software), dental radiography (sensor/plate model), ultrasound model.
12. Anesthesia: machine models, vaporizer agents, circuits stocked, multiparameter monitor models, ventilator (if any).
13. Infusion: fluid pump and syringe pump models.
14. Dental: delivery unit model, scaler/polisher, instruments.
15. Autoclave model; sterility-indicator system in use.
16. Crash cart: location(s), current contents list, who audits it and how often.

## D. Protocols and policy
17. Existing written protocols (surgical prep, anesthesia monitoring, cleaning/disinfection, controlled substances). Attach copies.
18. Hospital policy on assistant IM/IV-push medication administration under the A.R.S. § 32-2201(8)/(11) supervision definitions — what does CVAH permit assistants to do, and under which supervision level? These are higher-risk rows requiring current Board-rule and CVAH-policy verification; they are NOT settled by the § 32-2211(5) exemption alone.
19. Restraint policy limits (max people per patient, sedation-first thresholds, muzzle policy).
20. Controlled-substance logging procedure and storage.
21. Radiation safety: dosimetry badges? Who holds the program?
22. Isolation protocol for infectious cases.

## E. Training operations
23. Target number of simultaneous trainees.
24. Preferred device for assessors in treatment (phone/tablet) — drives assessor-interface deployment choice.
25. Budget ceiling for LMS hosting per month.
26. Any union/HR constraints on assessment documentation retention.

Deliver answers to the Program Administrator; they update `19_Equipment_Registry/equipment_registry.csv`, `02_Scope_and_Regulatory/Arizona_Scope_Matrix.csv` (hospital-policy column), and the manifest.
