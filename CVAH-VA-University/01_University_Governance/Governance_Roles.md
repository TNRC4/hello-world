# Governance and Operational Roles

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17

The University requires six operational roles. These are role definitions with tooling — **not** a leadership curriculum.

## 1. Program Administrator
Owns: enrollment, LMS administration, manifest maintenance, analytics reporting, revalidation calendar, asset register. Typically the practice manager or delegate. Tools: manager dashboard, manifest, LMS admin.

## 2. Medical Reviewer (DVM)
Owns: clinical accuracy of all content; approval of every Controlled Reference Card; sign-off on any MAJOR content revision; final authority on competency disputes. Must be an Arizona-licensed veterinarian. Tools: medical review workflow (`Medical_Review_Workflow.md`), controlled-reference register.

## 3. Supervising Veterinarian
The licensed veterinarian responsible under A.R.S. § 32-2281 for care rendered by assistants on shift. Confirms supervision-level requirements in the scope matrix are honored in daily assignment. Signs VA-level advancements and specialty credentials jointly with an assessor.

## 4. Supervising Technician (CVT preferred)
Day-to-day clinical mentor. Identifies practice opportunities during shifts, provides coached repetitions, records case-log entries, flags learners for practical assessment readiness.

## 5. Assessor
Conducts standardized practical assessments using the rubrics in `15_Practical_Competencies/`. May be a CVT or DVM; for safety-critical skills (venipuncture, IV catheterization, medication preparation, surgical prep, anesthesia-adjacent skills) the assessor must be a CVT or DVM. An assessor may not perform the final assessment of a learner they primarily trained on that skill when another qualified assessor is available (bias control; waive with Medical Reviewer approval in small-staff situations).

## 6. Manager
Owns workforce readiness: reviews the readiness dashboard, approves workload assignment consistent with passport states, initiates remediation conversations, co-signs Core graduation.

## Separation of powers
- Theory completion: recorded automatically by LMS.
- Practical competency: only an Assessor can grant.
- Progression to "Independently Assignable Within Hospital Policy": requires Assessor + Supervising Veterinarian or Medical Reviewer.
- Restriction/revocation: any DVM immediately; Assessor may impose "Temporarily Restricted" pending DVM review within 48 h.
- Remediation assignment: Assessor or Manager; remediation content per `23_Remediation/`.

## Assessment integrity rules
1. No self-assessment for any practical competency.
2. Rubrics must be scored during or immediately after observation, never from memory the next day.
3. "Seems pretty good at it" is not an assessment; only observable rubric behaviors count.
4. Critical-fail behaviors end the attempt regardless of other scores.
5. All records are retained per HR policy `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: retention period]`.
