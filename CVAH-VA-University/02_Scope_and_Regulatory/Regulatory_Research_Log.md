**Sourcing rule added after the 2026-08 defect:** statutory citations must be verified against primary text at azleg.gov (or the Board's published statute PDFs) and the verification date recorded here. Secondary compilations, mirrors, and summaries may inform but may never be the sole source for a section number. Quoting a rule correctly while citing the wrong section is a citation defect, not a rounding error — in a compliance document it is the kind of error that discredits the whole matrix.

Rules: every scope-matrix row must trace to a row here. Re-verify annually and on any Board rulemaking notice.
