# Scope Classification System

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17

Every task in the University carries exactly one scope class and one supervision requirement. These appear in the skill spec, the lesson, the rubric, and the passport. Learners are taught their own classifications explicitly (Week 0).

## Scope classes
| Code | Class | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| K | Educational knowledge only | Learner understands it; never performs it |
| O | May observe | May be present and watch |
| A | May assist | May support a DVM/CVT performing the task |
| P | May perform under specified supervision | May perform once passport state permits, under the stated supervision level |
| HP | Hospital-policy dependent | Legal under supervision, but CVAH policy decides; requires explicit policy entry |
| CT | Credentialed-technician restricted | Reserved to CVT (or DVM) by law, rule, or CVAH policy |
| DVM | Veterinarian restricted | Diagnosis, prognosis, prescription, surgery, and anything CVAH reserves to DVMs |
| JD | Jurisdiction dependent | Classification differs by state; Arizona classification given, flagged for any multi-state reuse |

## Supervision levels (Arizona-consistent terminology)
| Code | Level | Working definition |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Immediate/direct — veterinarian or CVT physically present and directly overseeing the task | Training states 4–6; all first performances |
| S2 | Direct — supervisor on premises and quickly available | Default for most granted technical competencies |
| S3 | Indirect — supervisor has given instructions and is available for communication, not necessarily on premises | Only where the scope matrix row explicitly allows and CVAH policy permits |

Under A.R.S. § 32-2281, a veterinary assistant employed by a licensed veterinarian may perform duties **other than diagnosis, prognosis, prescription, or surgery** under the direct or indirect supervision of the veterinarian, who is responsible for the assistant's performance. The University's language never uses "independent" in a legal sense; the highest passport state is **"Independently Assignable Within Hospital Policy,"** which always sits inside a defined supervision level.

## Non-negotiable exclusions (always DVM)
Diagnosis · prognosis · prescribing/selecting/altering treatment plans · surgery · any procedure Arizona law or Board rule reserves to veterinarians. Nothing in this University implies otherwise, and completing the University changes no one's legal scope.
