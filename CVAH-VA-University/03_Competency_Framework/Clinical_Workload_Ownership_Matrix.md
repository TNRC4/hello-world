# Clinical Workload Ownership Matrix

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Machine-readable: `Clinical_Workload_Ownership_Matrix.csv`

Purpose: make the transfer of work measurable. Each row states exactly what task moves onto the assistant's plate after sign-off, who was carrying it before, what supervision remains, and what invalidates the assignment. Managers use this with the readiness dashboard to plan staffing.

| Competency (skill ID) | Task transferred | Previously carried by | Supervision after sign-off | Prerequisites | Assignment invalid when | Escalation triggers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TPR (TS-ASM-001) | Obtain and document routine TPR on stable patients; report abnormals against CRC-001 before DVM sees patient | CVT (≈3–5 min/patient) | S2 | VA1 handling skills | Patient flagged fractious/critical; learner in state 11/13 | Any value outside CRC-001 bands; pain on handling; distress during acquisition |
| Restraint (TS-RES-001/002/003) | Provide restraint for exams, injections, blood draws — frees a second CVT | CVT or DVM pulled from other work | S2 | VA1 | Aggressive patient beyond trained level; sedated-patient positioning not yet signed | Escalating fear signs; airway compromise in restraint; staff-safety risk |
| Cephalic venipuncture (TS-VEN-CEPH-001) | Routine canine/feline blood collection for in-house and reference labs | CVT (≈5–10 min/draw incl. interruptions) | S2 (first 10 post-sign-off draws S1 per policy) | VA2 knowledge + reps met | >2 failed attempts on a patient; fragile/dehydrated flagged patients until VA3 | Hematoma forming; patient stress escalating; 2-attempt limit reached |
| Saphenous/jugular venipuncture (TS-VEN-SAPH/JUG-001) | Alternate-site draws incl. cats and small dogs; jugular volume draws (VA3) | CVT | S2 (jugular S1 until VA3) | Cephalic signed | Coagulopathy suspected (jugular); respiratory-compromised cats | Same as cephalic + respiratory distress in restraint |
| IV catheter (TS-IVC-CEPH-001) | Routine peripheral catheter placement for surgery/fluids | CVT (≈10–15 min/placement) | S1→S2 per scope row SCOPE-004 | VA2; venipuncture signed; policy permits | Critical patients; difficult-access patients until VA3; policy restriction | No flash ×2 attempts; vein blown; patient decompensating |
| SQ injection (TS-INJ-SQ-001) | Administer CVT/DVM-verified SQ meds & vaccines drawn and checked per five-rights workflow | CVT | S2 | VA2; med-math ≥90% | Any dose not independently verified; controlled substances | Injection-site reaction; patient collapse; dose discrepancy found |
| Oral/topical meds (TS-MED-PO-001) | Administer verified PO/topical/ophthalmic/otic meds to hospitalized patients | CVT | S2 | VA1 + med foundations | Dysphagic/regurgitating patients (PO); unverified orders | Refusal + stress; vomiting post-dose; wrong-label discovery |
| Fluid line mgmt (TS-FLU-001/002) | Set up, prime, connect approved fluid systems; monitor lines; change bags | CVT (setup ≈5–8 min; checks hourly) | S2 | VA2 | Additive-medicated fluids unless CVT-verified; syringe-pump drugs | Infiltration signs; pump alarms unresolved in 2 min; line disconnection |
| Sample processing (TS-SAM-001, TS-LAB-001/002) | Spin, separate, smear, PCV/TS, label, package for reference lab | CVT (≈10 min/panel) | S2 | VA2 | QC failures uninvestigated; unlabeled samples found | Grossly hemolyzed/clotted sample; analyzer error codes |
| Surgical prep (TS-SUR-PREP-001) | Clip and initial skin prep of routine surgical patients per protocol | CVT (≈10–15 min/patient) | S2 (patient under DVM/CVT anesthesia care) | Core Weeks 5–6 + sign-off | Contaminated/compromised skin; emergency surgeries until VA3 | Skin lesions found; patient too light/deep flagged by monitor team |
| Sterile opening (TS-SUR-STER-001) | Open packs/gloves/suture to sterile field without contamination | CVT | S2 | Asepsis module | Learner unsure of sterility — automatic stop-and-declare | Any suspected contamination (declare immediately — never conceal) |
| Radiographic positioning (TS-IMG-001) | Position and restrain for routine views; drive retake rate down | CVT + DVM time on retakes | S2 | Radiation safety module; dosimetry issued | Pregnant-staff restrictions per policy; sedation cases until signed | Patient distress; inability to achieve view in 2 attempts; equipment fault |
| Inpatient monitoring (TS-INP-001) | Scheduled observations, treatment-sheet execution, I/O tracking, escalation of changes | CVT (rounds time) | S2 | VA2 + Inpatient basics | ICU-level patients until Inpatient Academy; isolation without training | Any CRC-001 breach; pain score rise; line/bandage abnormalities |
| Emergency support (TS-EMR-001) | Crash-cart readiness checks; CPR support roles (compressions, recorder, runner) | CVT | S1 during events | Core emergency foundations | CPR lead/drug roles — never assistant's | Arrest recognition → immediate CPR activation call |

**Measurability rule:** each row feeds two analytics (`22_Analytics/Analytics_Specification.md`): (1) % of the task's weekly volume performed by signed-off assistants; (2) technician minutes/week returned. Generic claims ("helps with surgery") are prohibited in future rows.
