# Core Graduation Requirements

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17

**CVAH VA Core Graduate** is granted only when ALL nine gates are met. Online completion alone never equals technical clearance. No gate is waived for calendar time served.

| # | Gate | Standard | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knowledge | All required Core modules passed ≥80% | LMS gradebook |
| 2 | Safety | All safety-critical assessments passed at 100% (retake permitted after remediation; the safety-critical items themselves must all be answered correctly on the passing attempt) | LMS + item flags |
| 3 | Technical skill | All VA2-required practical competencies at state ≥8; restraint/TPR/sanitation at ≥9 | Passport |
| 4 | Repetition | Repetition requirements met per `16_Case_Logs/Repetition_Requirements.md`, across the required patient-type spread | Case logs |
| 5 | Recognition | Recognition battery ≥85%, including all critical-recognition items correct | 13_Assessments/Recognition_Battery |
| 6 | Communication | Two logged real or simulated abnormal-finding reports rated satisfactory on the SBAR-V rubric | PA-COM-001 |
| 7 | Documentation | Documentation audit of 10 consecutive real entries: zero patient-ID errors, ≤1 minor correction | Audit form |
| 8 | Integrated practical | Core Graduation Practical: pass ≥11 of 13 stations incl. ALL five safety-critical stations, zero critical fails | `15_Practical_Competencies/Core_Graduation_Practical_Stations.md` |
| 9 | Readiness review | Manager + Supervising Veterinarian + lead Assessor sign the readiness review form | Signed form |

**Timing:** target Weeks 6–8. Early graduation permitted any time all gates pass. If gates remain unmet at Week 8: structured gap-closure plan (Week 8 lesson W8-02), reassessment scheduled; outer window ~16 weeks triggers a Manager/Medical Reviewer conference — continuation, role redesign, or employment decision per HR policy. Remediation is normal and unstigmatized; the passport shows current state, not a count of tries, on the learner-facing view (full history remains in the audit log).
