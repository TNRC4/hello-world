# Specialty Academy Prerequisite Map

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17

All specialties require **CVAH VA Core Graduate** status. Additional prerequisites:

| Academy | Extra prerequisites | Rationale | Typical duration | Credential revalidation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advanced Perioperative & Anesthesia | VA2+; TS-SUR-PREP-001 & TS-SUR-STER-001 at ≥9; TS-IVC-CEPH-001 ≥5 where policy permits; recognition battery ≥90% on physiologic items | Most academically demanding; monitoring judgment builds on strong fundamentals | 8–12 weeks part-time | 24 months (monitoring practical) |
| Laboratory & IDEXX | TS-SAM-001, TS-LAB-001/002 at ≥8 | Preanalytics before analyzers | 4–6 weeks | 24 months (QC practical) |
| Veterinary Dentistry Support | Imaging Academy radiation-safety module (shared unit) passed; TS-RES-* ≥9 | Dental radiography + anesthetized-patient positioning | 5–8 weeks | 24 months |
| Diagnostic Imaging | Radiation-safety module 100%; dosimetry issued; TS-RES-* ≥9 | Legal/safety gate before exposure work | 4–6 weeks | 12 months (radiation safety refresher) |
| Emergency & Critical Care Support | TS-EMR-001 ≥8; TPR/restraint ≥9; RECOVER-aligned CPR module 100% | Speed depends on automatic fundamentals | 6–8 weeks | 12 months (CPR skills refresh per RECOVER cadence) |
| Inpatient & Hospitalized Patient Care | TS-INP-001 ≥8; TS-FLU-001 ≥9; TS-MED-PO-001 ≥9 | Trend recognition atop reliable task execution | 4–6 weeks | 24 months |
| Pharmacy & Medication Handling | Med-math screen ≥95%; TS-INJ-SQ-001 ≥8; zero medication documentation errors in last audit | Error-prevention culture prerequisite | 4–6 weeks | 24 months (controlled-substance module 12 months) |

**Cross-credit:** the Radiation Safety unit is shared between Imaging and Dentistry (complete once). The Anesthesia Academy's recovery module cross-credits to Inpatient's postoperative unit. Concurrent enrollment: max one specialty at a time alongside full clinical duties (Manager may approve two for VA3 staff).
