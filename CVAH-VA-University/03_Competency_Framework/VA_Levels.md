# VA1 / VA2 / VA3 Competency Level Standards

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17

VA levels are **competency milestones inside the University**, not separate courses. A level is granted when its full standard is met — never because time passed. Typical (not required) timing: VA1 ≈ end of Week 2; VA2 ≈ Weeks 5–6; Core Graduate ≈ Weeks 6–8; VA3 is post-core.

---

## VA1 — Foundational Assistant
*Can safely function in the hospital environment and perform basic patient-care and workflow responsibilities.*

**Knowledge (all passed at ≥80%, safety-critical items 100%):** hospital safety/OSHA module; scope & conduct module; infection control/PPE/sharps; terminology & hospital flow; patient identification.

**Practical competencies at state ≥8 (Practical Assessment Passed):**
TS-HND-001 safe approach/low-stress handling · TS-RES-001 lateral restraint · TS-RES-002 sternal/standing restraint · TS-ASM-001 TPR · TS-SAN-001 cleaning/disinfection · TS-DOC-001 basic documentation.

**Recognition:** passes the VA1 recognition check (identify unsafe restraint, fear escalation, dropped-sharp response, wrong-patient scenario).

**Workload unlocked (see Workload Ownership Matrix):** room turnover, patient movement, restraint for routine procedures, routine TPR with reporting, sanitation program participation, accurate basic records.

---

## VA2 — Technical Assistant
*Reliably performs a broader set of approved technical procedures and assumes meaningful clinical workload under required supervision.*

**Prerequisites:** VA1 granted.

**Knowledge:** all Week 2–4 modules passed; medication-math screen ≥90%; safety-critical items 100%.

**Practical competencies at state ≥8, with repetition requirements met (see `16_Case_Logs/Repetition_Requirements.md`):**
TS-VEN-CEPH-001 cephalic venipuncture · TS-VEN-SAPH-001 saphenous venipuncture · TS-INJ-SQ-001 SQ injection · TS-LAB-001 blood smear · TS-LAB-002 PCV/TS · TS-SAM-001 sample handling/tubes · TS-FLU-001 fluid line setup/priming · TS-MED-PO-001 oral medication administration · TS-RES-003 venipuncture positioning (holder role).
Plus, where CVAH policy permits assistants (scope matrix rows SCOPE-004/006): TS-IVC-CEPH-001 IV catheter at state ≥5 (practicing with direct supervision) and TS-INJ-IM-001 at state ≥8.

**Recognition:** passes VA2 recognition battery (hemolysis/clot causes, infiltration signs, "when to stop sticking," collapsing vein response, medication five-rights failure scenarios).

**Workload unlocked:** routine blood draws under defined supervision; sample processing to lab-ready state; SQ injections of CVT/DVM-verified doses; fluid line setup; oral medications; treatment-room support that no longer requires a CVT shadow.

---

## VA3 — Advanced Assistant (post-core)
*Advanced technical ability, strong situational awareness, workflow ownership, abnormality recognition, and selected advanced competencies.*

**Prerequisites:** CVAH VA Core Graduate; ≥3 months post-graduation clinical performance; no active remediation.

**Standard (all of):**
1. IV catheterization at state ≥9 (competent under defined supervision) with logged repetition across ≥3 patient categories — where policy permits; otherwise an approved substitution from the advanced list below.
2. Jugular venipuncture (TS-VEN-JUG-001) at state ≥8.
3. At least **one completed specialty academy credential**.
4. Advanced recognition battery pass (multi-parameter deteriorating-patient cases, ≥85%).
5. Workflow ownership attestation: two supervising CVTs/DVMs independently attest (structured form, `15_Practical_Competencies/VA3_Attestation_Form.md`) that the assistant anticipates needs, self-assigns appropriately, and escalates correctly.
6. Manager + Supervising Veterinarian co-sign.

Advanced substitution list (any one, if IV catheter restricted by policy): anesthesia monitoring support sign-off (Anesthesia Academy PA-ANES-MON-001) · full imaging positioning portfolio (PA-IMG-001 all views) · emergency readiness lead (PA-ECC-001 + crash-cart custodian).

**Specialty credentials sit on top of levels** — e.g., "VA3 — Advanced Assistant; Specialty Credentials: Advanced Perioperative & Anesthesia, Diagnostic Imaging." No VA3 is required to complete every specialty.
