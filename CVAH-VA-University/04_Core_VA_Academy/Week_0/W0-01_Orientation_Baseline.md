# W0-01 — Orientation, Baseline & Your Competency Passport

**Why this matters:** We train to competency, not to calendar. Today we find out where you're starting from, so we never waste your time re-teaching what you can already prove — and never assume anything you can't yet prove.

**Objectives:** explain how the University works (levels, passport states, three questions of competence); complete the diagnostic pretest honestly; complete the prior-experience inventory; know your Week-1 schedule.

**Core instruction:** How you graduate: nine gates, none of them "time served" (walk through `Graduation_Requirements` in learner language). The Competency Passport: your skills move through named states — you can always see exactly where you stand and what evidence moves you forward. The Three Questions: *Can you explain it? Can you do it? Can you recognize when it's going wrong?* All three, for every skill, always. Remediation here is normal and unstigmatized — extra practice is how motor skills work, not a verdict on you. The 2-attempt rule and stop-calling: anyone can call "stop" on any procedure, including you, including on your first day, including on the medical director.

**Activities today:** 1) Diagnostic pretest (`13_Assessments/Diagnostic_Pretest.md`) — scores route your personalized plan; guessing well helps nobody, least of all you. 2) Prior-experience inventory: list clinical skills you've performed before, where, roughly how many times. Anything listed can be **challenge-tested** — demonstrate it, skip the training. Claimed experience is verified, never grandfathered. 3) Passport walkthrough with the Program Administrator. 4) Facility tour with map handout: crash cart location (memorize today), exits, eyewash, sharps containers, isolation, O2 shutoffs.

**Knowledge check:** 1) What graduates you — time or gates? (*Gates: all nine, whenever you meet them.*) 2) You listed 200 blood draws at a previous job. What happens? (*You can challenge-test: pass the knowledge check ≥90% and the practical assessment directly.*) 3) Who may call "stop" during a procedure? (*Anyone. No rank in stop-calls.*)

**Competency connection:** passport initialized; all skills → state 1–2. **Key takeaways:** competency-based; passport is yours; honesty at baseline buys you speed. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
