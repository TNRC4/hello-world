# W1-01 — Hospital Flow & the Language of the Building

**Why this matters:** You become useful the day you can follow a normal conversation in treatment without stopping it.

**Objectives:** trace a patient's path (check-in → room → treatment → diagnostics → discharge/hospitalization); define the ~60 highest-frequency terms/abbreviations; read the whiteboard/schedule; know who does what (DVM/CVT/VA/CSR lanes).

**Core instruction:** Walk the patient journey with a floor map: what the CSR hands off, what happens in the room (history, exam, plan), how orders become treatment-area tasks, where diagnostics flow (lab bench, imaging), how hospitalization vs discharge branches. **Terminology core set (full glossary appendix + flashcard deck INT-01):** TPR, NPO, BAR/QAR, ADR, SQ/IM/IV/PO/OU-OS-OD/AU-AS-AD, SID/BID/TID/QID (and why "SID" confuses human pharmacists), CBC/chem/UA, PCV/TS, cysto, rads, sx, dental prophy, pre-med, induction, recovery, recheck, e-collar, DOA/CPR/DNR code status, hx/dx/tx/rx (and which of those are never yours). **Roles:** the delegation chain — DVM owns diagnosis/plan; CVT executes advanced delegated care; you execute your passport-granted skills; CSR owns client flow. You take clinical direction from DVM/CVT, schedule direction from your manager.

**Common mistakes:** guessing at an abbreviation instead of asking (a guessed "SID vs BID" is a med error waiting); answering client medical questions in the hallway. **What doesn't look right?** An order that seems wrong for the patient in front of you (giant-dog dose on a kitten chart) — asking beats assuming, every time.

**Knowledge check:** 1) NPO means? (*Nothing by mouth.*) 2) TID dosing is how many hours apart? (*~8.*) 3) A treatment says "0.5 mL SQ" but you can't read the drug. Action? (*Ask the writer; never guess or administer unverified.*)

**Practical assignment:** shadow a full appointment cycle; produce your own flow sketch; terminology deck to 90%. **Competency connection:** foundation for TS-DOC-001, TS-COM-001. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
