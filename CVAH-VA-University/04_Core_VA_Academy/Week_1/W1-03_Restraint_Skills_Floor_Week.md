# W1-03 — Restraint Floor Week: Lateral, Sternal, Standing, Positioning

**Why this matters:** This is your first "workload transfer" week: a signed-off restrainer frees a technician at every single procedure.

**Objectives:** perform lateral, sternal, and standing restraint to spec; begin venipuncture-positioning holds; integrate stop-calling.

**Core instruction:** This is a floor-first lesson: the content IS the skill specs — read **TS-RES-001, TS-RES-002, TS-RES-003** before the floor block. Online portion covers only the decision layer: *which position for which task* (lateral: rads, saphenous draws, some wound care; sternal: cephalic draws, jugular in cats, exams, most feline anything; standing: temp, SQ, abdominal palpation) and *position risk profiles* (lateral = worst for dyspnea; jugular positioning = airway watch; sternal = default-safe). Load-sharing with the phlebotomist: the holder owns the patient's comfort, airway watch, and the occlusion; the phlebotomist owns the stick; either can call stop.

**Troubleshooting live (coached):** the sit-collapse in standing restraint; the head-whip in sternal; the paddle-escape in lateral — each drilled with an instructor-played "patient" before real patients.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Dyspneic cat needs a blood sample — which positions are OFF the table without DVM input? (*Lateral and any jugular extension; minimal-restraint sternal/medial saphenous conversation with CVT/DVM first.*) 2) During a hold the phlebotomist says "release" — what exactly do you release? (*Occlusion only; restraint continues until the needle is out and site pressure established.*)

**Practical assignment (this week's floor targets):** RES-001 ≥5 canine + ≥3 feline coached; RES-002 ≥5 each posture; RES-003 ≥4 coached holds. Log everything. **Competency connection:** targets state 5–7 by Friday; PA-RES-001 scheduled Week 2. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
