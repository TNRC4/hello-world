# W1-04 — Sanitation, Infection Control, PPE & Sharps in Practice

**Why this matters:** Hospital-acquired infection travels on hands, mops, and shortcuts. Turnover speed with NO cross-contamination is a real skill the hospital feels immediately.

**Objectives:** perform exam-room turnover to spec; explain clean-before-disinfect and contact time; don/doff isolation PPE in order; manage laundry/waste streams.

**Core instruction:** Delivered with **TS-SAN-001** (read first). Additions: **hand hygiene doctrine** — between every patient, after gloves, before clean tasks; gloves replace nothing. **PPE order:** don gown → mask/eye pro (per case) → gloves; doff gloves → hygiene → gown → hygiene (doffing is where self-contamination happens — slow is clean). **Waste streams:** sharps / biohazard / regular / laundry (heavily soiled bagged at source); cytotoxic-drug handling awareness (Pharmacy Academy deepens). **The pathogen tier concept:** everyday organisms vs protocol-changers (parvo, panleuk, ringworm, kennel-cough cluster, lepto urine) — you don't diagnose them, you RECOGNIZE the flag on the record/board and switch workflows.

**What doesn't look right?** Isolation door propped; mop bucket touring the whole building; spray-wipe-done in 4 seconds; parvo-suspect walked through the lobby (*should have been carried/side-door per protocol*).

**Clinical case:** Puppy with vomiting/diarrhea was roomed in exam 2 before parvo suspicion was raised. You cleaned it "normal." It's now 2 hours later and the board says parvo-positive. What now? (*Report immediately; room out of service; full protocol-level clean with designated agent + contact time; trace what else you touched: door handles, scale, leash hooks; hygiene audit of yourself; no shame — but the earlier the report the smaller the trace.*)

**Knowledge check (100%):** 1) Disinfectant works only when? (*Applied to a pre-cleaned surface for full labeled contact time.*) 2) Doffing order? (*Gloves → hand hygiene → gown → hygiene [mask/eye per protocol].*) 3) Which common pathogen laughs at quats? (*Parvovirus [and ringworm spores] — designated agent required.*)

**Practical assignment:** 3 timed-but-scored room turnovers; 1 supervised isolation don/doff; find every sharps container on the map from memory. **Competency connection:** TS-SAN-001 → state 7; TS-SAN-002 → state 5. **References:** disinfectant labels; AAHA infection control; hospital SOP. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
