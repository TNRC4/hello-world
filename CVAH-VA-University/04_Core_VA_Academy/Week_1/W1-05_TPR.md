# W1-05 — TPR: Your First Clinical Measurement

**Why this matters:** TPR is the vital-sign backbone of every exam, every hospitalization, every anesthesia recovery. When your numbers are trusted, DVMs act on them — that's real transferred workload.

**Objectives:** acquire T, P (with quality/rhythm), R (with character) to spec; assess MM/CRT; recognize and report out-of-band values; document properly.

**Core instruction:** Read **CSC-ASM-01** (the teaching course; TS-ASM-001 governs) first; floor blocks daily this week. Online adds the *why*: what temperature does across conditions (fever vs hyperthermia vs the stress-bump); why pulse quality and synchrony matter (a pulse deficit means beats aren't perfusing); respiratory character as the earliest free warning (effort/abdominal push/noise beat the number); the stress-artifact problem (count R first, from distance; note purring/panting). Normal bands come from card **CRC-001** (posted; DVM-approved) — the lesson teaches you to USE the card, not memorize folklore.

**Visual/interactive:** heart & lung sound audio set INT-09 (count-along drills with answer reveal); CRT technique video clip in VID-ASM-001.

**What doesn't look right?** HR 60 in a stressed cat in hospital (*that's not calm, that's concerning — report*); "panting" recorded as R=200; temp 105.2°F charted at 9am and nobody told anyone until rounds (*out-of-band = report NOW*).

**Clinical case:** Post-op dachshund, recovery check: T 96.8°F, shivering, QAR. Junior assistant charts it and moves on. What was missed? (*Hypothermia is an active recovery problem — warming + report; recovery TPRs are action items, not paperwork.*)

**Knowledge check:** 1) Why count R before handling? (*Handling/stress elevates rate — you'd record an artifact.*) 2) Pulse deficit means? (*Heartbeats without corresponding femoral pulses — report.*) 3) Where do CVAH's normal bands live? (*CRC-001 card — versioned, DVM-approved.*)

**Practical assignment:** ≥10 coached TPRs this week (≥3 feline), values cross-checked against trainer's within tolerance. **Competency connection:** TS-ASM-001 → state 6–7; PA-ASM-TPR-001 target early Week 2. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
