# W1-06 — Documentation & Reporting: If It Isn't Written, It Didn't Happen

**Why this matters:** Your observations only become medicine when they land in the record and in the right ear, in time.

**Objectives:** write compliant treatment entries in the PIMS; use approved abbreviations only; deliver an SBAR-V verbal report; know what never goes in a record.

**Core instruction:** Read **TS-DOC-001** and **TS-COM-001** (they are the content); PIMS click-path appendix `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: screenshots per house PIMS]`. Practice layer online: five entry makeovers (vague → compliant), e.g. "did meds" → "08:05 cephalexin 500 mg PO given in food, ate fully, no emesis by 08:30. —JG"; abbreviation quiz deck; SBAR-V builder interactive (INT-02: drag observation fragments into S/B/A/R/V slots, three scenarios).

**What doesn't look right (record edition)?** Entries timestamped in a neat block at 17:58; a treatment charted that you watched not happen (*that's the integrity cliff — report it, never mirror it*); diagnosis words in assistant entries ("pyometra suspect" vs the correct "purulent vaginal discharge noted, Dr. K notified 10:14").

**Knowledge check:** 1) When do you chart a treatment? (*At administration time — never before, never batch-later.*) 2) Correct fix for a wrong entry? (*Addendum per PIMS convention — never delete.*) 3) Build an SBAR-V from: post-op cat, gums pale, HR 200, was fine an hour ago. (*Model answer keyed in LMS: leads with patient/location, numbers, trend, "come now," closes loop.*)

**Practical assignment:** 5 real entries co-signed by trainer; 1 live SBAR-V on a real (even boring) finding. **Competency connection:** TS-DOC-001, TS-COM-001 → state 6–7; graduation gates 6–7 begin accumulating here. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
