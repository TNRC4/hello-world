# W2-02 — History Support & Clinical Observations

**Why this matters:** DVMs decide with data; half the data is history and observation. Assistants who capture clean histories and notice real changes multiply the doctor.

**Objectives:** collect a structured preliminary history without leading; quantify observations; distinguish observation from interpretation; recognize red-flag answers that shortcut to escalation.

**Core instruction:** The house history frame (mirrors PIMS template): presenting complaint (client's words) → duration/course (better/worse/same) → appetite/water (quantified: "half normal amount ×3 days") → V/D/coughing/sneezing (character, frequency, last event) → urination changes → meds on board incl. OTC/human meds ("anything from your medicine cabinet?" catches the ibuprofen) → diet incl. treats/table → environment (indoor/out, other pets, travel, toxin access) → vaccines/preventives current? Ask open then closed ("Tell me about his eating" then "how many meals did he finish yesterday?"); never lead ("He's not vomiting, right?" teaches clients to say no). **Red-flag answers that you escalate immediately, mid-history:** male cat straining in litterbox (*urinary obstruction until proven otherwise — this is a now-emergency*); unproductive retching in a big dog (*GDV concern*); "got into" antifreeze/xylitol/rodenticide/grapes/human meds; labored breathing at rest; collapse; pale gums noticed by you at any point.

**Observation vs interpretation drill:** "ataxic" is DVM language when you mean "wobbly in hind end, crossed legs twice walking to scale" — write what you saw; the pattern-name belongs to the doctor.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Client mentions their male cat "keeps going to the box, nothing comes out." Your move? (*Interrupt politely, alert DVM/CVT immediately — possible obstruction, time-critical.*) 2) Why are leading questions banned? (*They contaminate the data — clients agree with authority.*) 3) Rewrite as observation: "dog is painful in abdomen." (*"Tensed and vocalized when belly touched during positioning."*)

**Practical assignment:** 4 supervised histories; trainer scores the red-flag drill with 6 scripted client statements. **Competency connection:** TS-COM-001; recognition battery items seeded here. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
