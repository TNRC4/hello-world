# W2-03 — Medication Foundations: Rights, Labels, Routes, Math

**Why this matters:** Medication error is the most preventable serious-harm class in the building — and prevention is mostly performed at YOUR step.

**Objectives:** recite and apply the five rights + documentation/response; read every field of a drug label; name routes and their abbreviations; perform the three core calculations; state your absolute limits (never select, never alter, never unverified).

**Core instruction:** Read **TS-MED-001** (the workflow spec). This lesson builds the knowledge layer: **label anatomy** (drug/generic vs brand; CONCENTRATION as the danger line — 5 mg/mL vs 50 mg/mL kills; expiry; storage; controlled-schedule marks). **Routes:** PO/SQ/IM/IV/topical/ophthalmic/otic/transdermal/rectal + why route changes effect and speed (educational only). **Math block (mastery ≥90% required for VA2):** (1) dose→volume: volume mL = dose mg ÷ concentration mg/mL; (2) tablets: tablets = dose ÷ strength (halves/quarters only when scored + order permits); (3) percent solutions awareness: X% = X g/100 mL (so 2% = 20 mg/mL) — you don't compound, you sanity-check. Estimation habit: before calculating, ballpark ("about 2-and-a-bit mL") — if calculator answer says 23 mL, your ballpark catches the slipped decimal. Worked set of 20 problems + INT-03 randomized drill bank (unlimited practice, instant explanations).

**What doesn't look right?** A syringe on the counter with no label; an order for "insulin 5 mL" (*insulin doses in UNITS with matched U-40/U-100 syringes — mismatch is a classic disaster; flag any unit-odd order*); two drugs with near-identical names shelved adjacent.

**Knowledge check (sample of the 20-problem set):** 1) Order 75 mg, stock 50 mg/mL → mL? (*1.5 mL.*) 2) Order 12.5 mg, tablets 25 mg scored → ? (*Half tablet.*) 3) 2% lidocaine = ? mg/mL (*20 — awareness only, assistants don't draw anesthetics.*) 4) Your calculated volume is 10× your ballpark. Next move? (*Stop; recheck concentration line; re-verify with CVT — never administer through doubt.*)

**Competency connection:** TS-MED-001 → state 3; math gate toward VA2. **References:** ISMP-adapted safety principles; Plumb's (educational paraphrase); house formulary. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
