# W2-04 — Sample Collection Basics: Urine, Fecal, Cytology Support

**Why this matters:** Diagnostics start with specimen quality. Ruined samples cost the client money, the patient time, and the team credibility.

**Objectives:** collect/receive free-catch urine properly; assist cysto positioning; handle fecal samples; prepare ear/skin cytology materials; label and store everything to spec.

**Core instruction:** **Urine:** free-catch mid-stream into clean container (client-brought "clean jars" get a fresh container transfer + a note); time collected matters (analysis within 30–60 min at room temp or refrigerate + note it — crystals and cells change with delay); first-morning concentrated sample preferred for some tests (per order). Cysto: DVM/CVT performs (scope class CT); YOUR role: positioning (dorsal/lateral per performer), restraint serenity, tube handoff. **Fecal:** fresh matters (motility/antigen), gram-size sample fine, refrigerate if delayed per lab directory, gloves + hygiene (zoonoses from W0-02 get real here). **Cytology support:** ear swabs — one swab per ear, LABELED per ear (AD/AS — sides differ, treatment differs); slides prepped per CVT direction (roll, don't smear-crush); skin: tape preps/impression smears staged, patient held still (the CVT's collection is only as good as your restraint). **Universal law:** label at patient-side; unlabeled = discard-level error (TS-SAM-001 doctrine extends to every specimen).

**What doesn't look right?** Urine sitting on the counter since morning submitted at 4pm as fresh; two ear swabs in one hand, unlabeled ("I remember which is which" — no); fecal loop sample when the order wanted antigen-test volume.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Why does urine age matter? (*Cells lyse, crystals form/dissolve, bacteria multiply — results shift within the hour at room temp.*) 2) Ear cytology from both ears: handling rule? (*Separate, labeled per ear at collection.*) 3) Your role in cysto? (*Positioning + restraint + supplies — the needle belongs to CVT/DVM.*)

**Practical assignment:** process 3 urine, 2 fecal, assist 2 cytology collections. **Competency connection:** TS-SAM-002/003 → 5–6. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
