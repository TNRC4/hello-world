# W3-02 — Cephalic Venipuncture: Model to Patient

**Why this matters:** The single highest-value technical transfer of the Core Academy starts this week.

**Objectives:** perform the full cephalic draw sequence on the model to fluency; transition to coached live draws; apply the 2-attempt limit and stop criteria as reflexes, not rules you remember later.

**Core instruction:** The teaching content is **CSC-VEN-01** (Clinical Skill Course — read it fully; it is the course) with **TS-VEN-CEPH-001** as the governance spec. Structure of the week: Day 1 — model lab (≥10 successful model sticks; trainer scores entry angle, flash response, aspiration pressure); Day 2 — observe 3 live draws with narration-by-trainer, then holder-role on real draws (you already have RES-003 — now feel the draw from the other side); Days 3–5 — coached live draws on selected easy patients (calm, good veins, non-urgent samples — the training board flags "learner-appropriate" cases at morning rounds). The mental checklist drilled until automatic: *ID → assemble → position → palpate (trust the finger) → stabilize → enter → flash → flatten → aspirate gently → release-occlusion-THEN-withdraw → pressure → transfer fast → label at side → sharps → recheck site.*

**The failure curriculum (deliberate):** trainer stages on the model: through-and-through, flash-then-wall, collapse-on-aspiration — you learn each failure's *feel* in silicone before meeting it in a patient. Recognition beats luck.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Flash then nothing — first two moves? (*Drop angle flat; advance 1–2 mm/rotate slightly; ease aspiration.*) 2) Define one "attempt." (*Needle fully exits skin = attempt over; a subcutaneous redirect is the same attempt.*) 3) After your second miss? (*Hand off. Full stop. No third stick — ever at this stage.*)

**Practical assignment:** the week's targets from the spec (§24). Every attempt logged, successful or not — unsuccessful logs are tuition, not shame. **Competency connection:** TS-VEN-CEPH-001 states 4→6. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
