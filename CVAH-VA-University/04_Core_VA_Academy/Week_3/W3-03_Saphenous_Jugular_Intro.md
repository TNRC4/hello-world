# W3-03 — Saphenous Draws & Jugular Introduction

**Why this matters:** Cephalic-only phlebotomists stall the moment a forelimb is catheter-reserved or a cat says no. Site flexibility is what makes you dependable.

**Objectives:** perform lateral saphenous (canine) and medial saphenous (feline) draws coached; explain jugular indications/contraindications and demonstrate the holder role; know why jugular sticks wait for your S1 sign-off later.

**Core instruction:** Read **TS-VEN-SAPH-001** and **TS-VEN-JUG-001** (jugular is theory + holder-role only this week; live jugular sticks are a supervised later milestone — VA3-track). Floor blocks: saphenous model work half-day, then coached feline medial saphenous with towel-wrapped patients (this is where your W1 towel skills pay rent). Jugular block: you hold for the CVT's jugular draws all week — the holder role IS jugular training: nose-up-not-hyperextended, airway watch, low occlusion feel.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Why is medial saphenous often the feline vessel of choice? (*Large, flat, accessible under low-stress towel restraint; spares neck/forelimbs.*) 2) Absolute assistant no-go for jugular? (*Suspected coagulopathy; severe dyspnea positioning; escalate instead.*) 3) The lateral saphenous rolls — the fix lives where? (*In your distal traction, not in more needle force.*)

**Practical assignment:** ≥3 coached saphenous draws (≥2 feline medial); ≥4 jugular holds. **Competency connection:** TS-VEN-SAPH-001 → 5–6; TS-VEN-JUG-001 → 3–4; TS-RES-003 → 7–8. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
