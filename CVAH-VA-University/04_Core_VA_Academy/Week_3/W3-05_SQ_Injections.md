# W3-05 — Subcutaneous Injections

**Why this matters:** Vaccines and SQ meds are constant, daily, high-volume tasks — a signed-off assistant absorbs dozens per week.

**Objectives:** perform verified SQ injections to spec; integrate five-rights choreography with a verifier; recognize and respond to injection reactions.

**Core instruction:** The content is **TS-INJ-SQ-001** + the TS-MED-001 verification choreography, now performed live: you prepare, the CVT verifies, you administer, you watch, you chart. Reaction module: normal transient sting vs the report-now set (facial swelling, hives, vomiting within minutes-to-hours, collapse = anaphylaxis pathway — locate the DVM FIRST, then help stage); vaccine-site conventions per house map; the through-and-through miss and its honest handling (wet fur → report → DVM decides, never silent re-dose).

**Knowledge check:** 1) Aspirate before SQ injection — why? (*Blood return means you're intravascular — wrong compartment; withdraw and restart.*) 2) Ten minutes post-vaccine, puppy's muzzle is swelling. (*DVM immediately — anaphylaxis pathway; do not "watch it" solo.*) 3) You suspect half the dose went through the tent. (*Report. The re-dose decision is not yours — double-dosing is the other cliff.*)

**Practical assignment:** ≥5 coached SQ (mix vaccine/med/fluids-under-skin where ordered). **Competency connection:** TS-INJ-SQ-001 → 6–7; PA scheduled Week 4–5. IM injections open in Week 4 after this is solid. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
