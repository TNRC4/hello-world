# W4-03 — IV Catheter Troubleshooting & Complication Recognition

**Why this matters:** Every hospitalized catheter WILL misbehave eventually on your shift. Question 3 of competence — recognizing wrong — is this whole lesson.

**Objectives:** run the no-flash / flash-then-fail / won't-thread decision trees; recognize infiltration, phlebitis, occlusion, and displacement from ward-round evidence; respond to each within scope.

**Core instruction:** **TS-IVC-CEPH-001** §§11–16 expanded into drills. The branching interactive INT-06: eight scenarios (e.g., "pump occlusion alarm, line traced clear, flush meets resistance, site soft" → aspirate check? positional test? report?) each with consequence-following ("you flushed hard against resistance — here's what that can do"). Ward-evidence gallery: photos/描述 of infiltration (cool doughy swelling, slowed drip, dry flash), phlebitis (warm, red-tracked, corded vessel, pain on flush — dwell-time context), displacement (wet wrap, blood backing up), chewed-line catastrophe (why e-collars and line-checks exist). Scope discipline: your verbs are STOP-the-pump, TRACE, ASSESS gently, REPORT with specifics — removal/replacement decisions belong to CVT/DVM (removal itself is delegated per TS-IVC-002 when granted).

**Clinical case:** 03:00, fluids "running," morning limb is twice the size, pump never alarmed. Reconstruct. (*Infiltrated hours ago; pump pressure happily pushed sub-q; hourly line-trace + site-palpation checks were the missing control — pumps don't know where the fluid GOES.*)

**Knowledge check:** 1) The pump is silent — is the line fine? (*Unknown. Pumps count volume, not destination; trace and palpate.*) 2) Flush meets resistance: next? (*Stop. Never force. Positional check, gentle aspirate, report.*) 3) Warm red cord along the vessel = ? and whose call is next? (*Phlebitis pattern — report to CVT/DVM; expect removal order.*)

**Practical assignment:** run 8/8 INT-06 scenarios ≥85%; perform 10 real catheter-site checks on ward rounds with trainer countersign. **Competency connection:** TS-IVC-002 → 6–7; recognition battery items. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
