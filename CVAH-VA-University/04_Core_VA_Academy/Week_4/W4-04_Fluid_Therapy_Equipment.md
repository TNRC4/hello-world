# W4-04 — Fluid Therapy Equipment: Bags, Lines, Priming, Rates

**Why this matters:** Fluid running rooms run on assistants. Setup speed WITH zero air and zero mis-rates is the deliverable.

**Objectives:** select correct components per order; prime air-free; compute drip rates (gravity fallback) and understand pump-rate verification; label everything; perform bag changes.

**Core instruction:** The content is **TS-FLU-001**; this lesson adds the *why* layer: crystalloid basics educationally (what LRS/0.9% NaCl/Plasma-Lyte-class fluids are FOR conceptually — replacement vs maintenance ideas; assistants never choose, but understanding makes your monitoring intelligent); macro vs micro set math with the worked table (e.g., 60 mL/h on a 15 gtt set = 15 gtt/min = 1 drop/4 s); why air discipline scales with patient size; additive-bag rules (CVT/DVM verified, agitate per label, BIG label). Lab block: prime perfect lines ×5 (trainer inspects the full length against light), stage a bag change mid-"infusion," pediatric micro-set setup.

**Knowledge check:** 1) 120 mL/h, 10 gtt/mL set → gtt/min? (*20.*) 2) Drip chamber flooded full — problem because? (*Can't count drops; also risks bolus if inverted — squeeze-refix to 1/3–1/2.*) 3) A bag labeled "+KCl 20 mEq" arrives pre-mixed from the CVT. Your checks? (*Verify against order with the CVT, confirm mixing/label completeness, hang with pump per policy — potassium-added fluids are never free-flowed.*)

**Practical assignment:** 5 primes, 1 change, 1 micro-set, all inspected. **Competency connection:** TS-FLU-001 → 6–7. **References:** AAHA fluid therapy guidelines (educational); set IFUs. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
