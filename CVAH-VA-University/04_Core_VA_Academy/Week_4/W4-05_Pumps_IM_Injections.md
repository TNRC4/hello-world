# W4-05 — Infusion Pumps, Syringe Pumps & IM Injections

**Why this matters:** Two closers for the technical block: the machines that meter fluids, and the injection route that demands the most respect.

**Objectives:** operate house pumps to spec incl. the alarm tree; prevent free-flow always; perform coached IM injections at approved sites.

**Core instruction — pumps:** the content is **TS-FLU-002**; lab block on house models with staged faults (trainer rigs occlusion, air, wrong-set). The free-flow catechism: clamp BEFORE door, every time, both directions. Alarm tree run live ×3. Syringe-pump block: brand/size menu match, seating, why syringe pumps carry the potent drugs (precision) and therefore why their alarms outrank your other tasks. **IM injections:** content is **TS-INJ-IM-001**; site map session on cadaver-model/diagram (epaxial default, quadriceps alternative, caudal thigh BANNED at CVAH — sciatic risk), aspiration as non-negotiable, S1 supervision for your first five.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Free-flow prevention rule? (*Roller clamp closed before the pump door opens — always.*) 2) Air-in-line alarm: the forbidden move? (*Opening the door and flicking bubbles toward the patient; clamp, disconnect per protocol, re-prime/replace.*) 3) Why is the caudal thigh banned? (*Sciatic nerve proximity — permanent deficit risk; epaxials preferred.*) 4) IM aspiration shows blood. (*Withdraw fully; new needle, new site; report if any drug was delivered.*)

**Practical assignment:** pump lab with 3 staged faults caught; ≥3 coached IM (S1). **Competency connection:** TS-FLU-002 → 6–7; TS-INJ-IM-001 → 5. VA2 candidacy check runs end of this week. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
