# W5-01 — Radiology Foundations & Radiation Safety

**Why this matters:** Imaging is daily bread — and the one place your invisible occupational dose accumulates for a career. Safety module is a 100% gate BEFORE any exposure-room work.

**Objectives:** pass radiation safety at 100%; explain ALARA in behaviors; wear/handle PPE and dosimetry correctly; begin positioning fundamentals (lateral/VD thorax & abdomen).

**Core instruction:** **Physics at working depth:** X-rays are ionizing — energy that breaks molecular bonds; dose is cumulative and invisible; scatter (the patient becomes a low-grade source during exposure — distance from the table edge matters, not just the primary beam). **ALARA behaviors:** minimize TIME in exposures (be elsewhere when not needed); maximize DISTANCE (step back at the console; use positioning aids so no one holds); SHIELD always (apron+thyroid every time, gloves only for edge-work, NOTHING in the primary beam ever — a leaded glove in-beam still takes serious dose and ruins the image); collimation as dose control; sedation/aids-first doctrine; pregnancy-declaration pathway `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: policy]`; dosimeter etiquette (collar, outside apron, never left on the apron hook, readings reviewed). Then **TS-IMG-001** positioning content begins: the four core views, landmarks, breathing timing, rotation self-check.

**Knowledge check (100% gate; sample):** 1) The three ALARA levers? (*Time, distance, shielding.*) 2) May a gloved hand steady a paw at the collimated edge? (*Edge-work with lead only per policy and never in primary beam — aids first; the goal is hands-free.*) 3) Where does your dosimeter live? (*On YOU at collar level outside the apron during work; stored off-apron otherwise.*) 4) Peak inspiration matters for which views? (*Thorax.*)
