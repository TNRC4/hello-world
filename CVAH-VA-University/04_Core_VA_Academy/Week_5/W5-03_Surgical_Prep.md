# W5-03 — Surgical Patient Prep: Clip & Scrub Week

**Why this matters:** Prep quality is infection control at the incision line — and prep SPEED (without quality loss) is anesthesia-time control.

**Objectives:** perform clip + initial scrub to spec on coached cases; verbalize site/side confirmation; protect patient temperature throughout.

**Core instruction:** Content is **CSC-SUR-01** (the teaching course; TS-SUR-PREP-001 governs) — read fully first. Lab then floor: Day 1 clipper handling on shelter-donation/grooming-case practice `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: practice-material policy]` — blade angle, tension, the burn-avoidance feel, blade-temp checks against your own wrist; Days 2–5 coached real preps on routine sx (spay/neuter/mass removal tier). The site/side liturgy performed OUT LOUD every time ("Bella Chen, left stifle — confirm?") to the anesthetist; margins per posted procedure cards; the center-out spiral discipline; pooling and hypothermia guarding (small patient = towel dams, warmed scrub per protocol).

**Knowledge check:** 1) Board says R, record says L. (*Nothing gets clipped until resolved with DVM. Nothing.*) 2) Clipper blade feels hot. (*Swap/cool — thermal burn; check patient skin, report any lesion found or made.*) 3) Gauze on pass 4 still comes away dirty at cycle minimum. (*Keep scrubbing — cycle counts are minimums; clean-gauze endpoint plus minimum, whichever is longer.*)

**Practical assignment:** ≥5 coached preps (≥2 procedure types) per spec §24. **Competency connection:** TS-SUR-PREP-001 → 5–7; PA-SUR-PREP-001 target Week 6–7. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
