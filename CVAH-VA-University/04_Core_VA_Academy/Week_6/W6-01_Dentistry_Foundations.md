# W6-01 — Dentistry Foundations for Assistants

**Why this matters:** Dental days are equipment-heavy, anesthesia-long, and workflow-sensitive — an oriented assistant keeps them humane and on schedule.

**Objectives:** describe the dental-day workflow; name basic oral anatomy and the Triadan shorthand at recognition level; set up/break down the dental station; state the anesthetized-dental-patient risks you help guard (hypothermia, airway water, positioning).

**Core instruction:** Workflow: anesthetized procedure (cleaning/probing/charting/rads → DVM assessment → extractions if needed by DVM) — nothing dental at CVAH happens on awake patients beyond exam-room looks. Oral anatomy for support work: incisors/canines/premolars/molars; Triadan hundreds (100s right upper → 400s right lower, quadrant logic only — charting depth lives in the Dentistry Academy); periodontal disease concept (plaque→calculus→gingivitis→attachment loss) so the procedure makes sense. Station setup: delivery unit (water lines charged, handpieces seated), scaler/polisher checks, prophy paste/cups, mouth props POLICY note (spring-loaded gags in cats = restricted per current safety consensus — house policy card), rad sensor sleeved, warming layers staged, suction ready. Your guard duties: thermal (long wet procedures chill patients — active warming per protocol, temp on the record), airway water awareness (throat packs/suction are CVT/DVM managed — you watch pooling and SAY SO), eye protection (lube per protocol, instruments never passed over the face).

**Knowledge check:** 1) Tooth 104 is which quadrant? (*Right upper — canine, as it happens.*) 2) Why are dental patients hypothermia-prone? (*Long anesthesia + continuous water + open mouth heat loss.*) 3) Awake "dental cleaning" requested by a client? (*Not performed — anesthesia-free scaling is cosmetic and harmful below the gumline; route the conversation to the DVM.*)

**Practical assignment:** set up and break down the station twice supervised; observe one full dental with checklist. **Competency connection:** TS-DEN-102 → 4–5; gateway to Dentistry Academy. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
