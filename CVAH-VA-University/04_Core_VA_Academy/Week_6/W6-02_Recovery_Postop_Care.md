# W6-02 — Recovery & Post-Operative Care

**Why this matters:** More anesthetic deaths cluster in recovery than under the drape. Recovery vigilance is an assistant superpower the data actually supports.

**Objectives:** run a recovery bay to spec; execute the recovery observation loop; recognize the red-flag set instantly; support smooth (not chaotic) emergence; chart recovery properly.

**Core instruction:** Bay standards: pre-warmed, dry bedding, quiet/dim where possible, suction+O2 reachable, the patient NEVER unobserved while extubation-adjacent. The loop (interval per orders, typically q5–15min early): breathing rate/effort/noise (stridor post-extubation = airway problem NOW), MM/CRT, temperature until threshold per orders, mentation trajectory (should climb steadily — plateau or dip = report), incision/bandage first-look, pain observation (house scale) vs dysphoria discussion (distinguishing them is the CVT/DVM's call — your job is to describe: "vocalizing, thrashing, not responsive to voice" vs "quiet, guarding incision, responds to name"). Positioning: sternal-supported when possible, brachycephalic heads-up-tongue-out doctrine, never flat-neck-flexed. The brachycephalic hour: these patients obstruct late — extended watch per orders. Feeding/water reintroduction per orders only.

**Clinical case:** Bulldog extubated 20 min ago, "recovered," left alone for cleanup. Found cyanotic. Rewind the tape. (*Never alone that early; head/neck positioning maintained; stridor precursors likely missed; recovery staffing is a scheduled duty, not spare-time.*)

**Knowledge check:** 1) Post-extubation stridor means? (*Airway narrowing/obstruction — CVT/DVM immediately; re-intubation supplies live in reach because of exactly this.*) 2) Mentation was climbing, now flat for 30 min. (*Report — recovery should trend one direction.*) 3) Cat post-op, T 96.5°F. (*Active warming per protocol + report; hypothermia deepens sedation and delays everything.*)

**Practical assignment:** 4 coached recoveries incl. 1 brachycephalic or high-watch case; charting audited. **Competency connection:** TS-SUR-003 → 7–8; feeds Inpatient + Anesthesia academies. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
