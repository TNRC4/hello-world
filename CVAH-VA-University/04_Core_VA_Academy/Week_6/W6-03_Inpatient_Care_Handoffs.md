# W6-03 — Inpatient Care Deepening & the Art of the Handoff

**Why this matters:** Week 2 made you safe in the ward; Week 6 makes you someone the ward can be LEFT with (routine tier), and someone whose handoffs don't drop patients.

**Objectives:** run a multi-patient observation round efficiently; manage I/O tracking; execute the full handoff protocol; escalate on trend, not just threshold.

**Core instruction:** Content anchors **TS-INP-001** at full depth now. Multi-patient round-craft: sheet-first planning (who's due what, batch the route), the per-kennel rhythm (ID→look-before-touch→bundle→chart-now), isolation LAST always. I/O: measure don't vibe (graduated bowls, litter weights where ordered, walk-pee estimates flagged as estimates). Trend escalation: the sheet's thresholds are floors, not the whole game — HR 120→132→144 over three checks is a REPORT even if the threshold is 150 (trajectory beats snapshot; this is the recognition question again). Handoff protocol (TS-INP-103 pattern): walk the ward WITH the sheet, per patient: status trend, due-next, watch-fors, lines/tubes inventory; incoming repeats back the watch-fors (closed loop); written handoff note per house format.

**Knowledge check:** 1) Threshold 150, readings 120→132→144. (*Report the trend now — don't wait to lose the race at 150.*) 2) Why isolation last? (*Traffic direction: clean→dirty, never back.*) 3) Handoff isn't complete until? (*Incoming has walked the ward with the sheet and repeated back the watch-fors.*)

**Practical assignment:** lead 2 rounds under observation; give + receive 2 protocol handoffs. **Competency connection:** TS-INP-001 → 7–8; VA2 candidacy closes this week for most learners. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
