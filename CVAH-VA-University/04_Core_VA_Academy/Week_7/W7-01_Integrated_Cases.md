# W7-01 — Integrated Case Series: Whole Patients, Whole Shifts

**Why this matters:** Weeks 1–6 taught skills in lanes. Real shifts braid them. Week 7 is deliberately about the braid.

**Objectives:** execute multi-skill sequences on real cases end-to-end; self-prioritize a task stack; maintain quality under mild time pressure; narrate your reasoning on request.

**Core instruction + structure:** Each day this week you carry a *case thread* under supervision: e.g., Day 1 — admit a sx patient: intake TPR → pre-op checklist → catheter place/assist per track → prep → recovery sit → discharge-support charting. Day 2 — the lab thread: 3 draws with full processing to lab-ready + QC awareness. Day 3 — the ward thread: lead rounds, meds, I/O, one handoff. Day 4 — the imaging/dental support thread. Day 5 — trainer-selected weak-area thread (your passport tells us where). Trainers score with the Integration Rubric (prioritization, transitions, asking-at-the-right-moment, patient-ID discipline under load, charting-in-the-moment).

**The prioritization frame (taught Monday, quizzed daily):** Patient-safety-now > time-critical diagnostics > comfort/pain-adjacent > routine schedule > housekeeping; interruptions re-sort the stack out loud ("finishing this flush, then I'm on it — or is it a now-thing?").

**Simulation tie-in:** SIM-CORE-001 (deteriorating ward patient embedded mid-shift — do you notice while busy?) runs unannounced once this week. That's the point.

**Knowledge check:** three prioritization scenarios (keyed in LMS bank QB-CORE-INT). **Competency connection:** evidence accumulation for gates 5–8. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
