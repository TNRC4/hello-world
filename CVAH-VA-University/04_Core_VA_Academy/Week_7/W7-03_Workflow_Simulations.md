# W7-03 — Workflow Simulations & Practical-Exam Rehearsal

**Why this matters:** The graduation practical shouldn't be the first time you feel the full-station format.

**Objectives:** complete a rehearsal circuit of all 13 graduation stations at practice intensity; identify and close remaining gaps with your trainer; rehearse the emergency simulation as a team.

**Structure:** Mock-station morning: all 13 stations (`15_Practical_Competencies/Core_Graduation_Practical_Stations.md`) run at practice weight — trainers coach DURING (unlike the real exam) and every station ends with the rubric shown and self-scored first ("how do YOU think that went?" calibrates self-assessment, which is itself scored). Team sim afternoon: SIM-CORE-004 emergency scenario with full role rotation. Gap-closure conference: you + trainer + passport → a written Week-8 plan: which stations are exam-ready, which get targeted reps, whether your graduation practical books for Week 7's end (accelerated), Week 8, or extends.

**Knowledge check:** none — this lesson IS assessment rehearsal. **Competency connection:** direct feed to gates 3, 4, 8. **Version:** 1.0 2026-08-17.
