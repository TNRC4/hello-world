# W8-01 — Graduation Week: Final Exam & Practical

**Why this matters:** This is the week you prove the three questions across the whole Core scope — and the hospital formally starts counting on you.

**Structure:** 1) **Final knowledge examination** (Form assembled from QB-CORE per `13_Assessments/Core_Final_Exam_Blueprint.md`): 75 items, blueprint-weighted, ≥80% overall + 100% on embedded safety-critical items; one retake path per `23_Remediation/` with targeted review first. 2) **Core Graduation Practical:** 13 stations, real equipment, standardized patients/models per station spec; pass ≥11/13 including ALL five safety-critical stations; zero critical fails (a critical fail anywhere = that station failed + targeted remediation before re-attempt regardless of total). 3) **Documentation & communication gates** audited from your accumulated logs (gates 6–7). 4) **Readiness review:** Manager + Supervising Veterinarian + lead Assessor, with your passport and case logs on the table; outcome is one of: GRADUATE (credential granted, workload matrix rows activate per passport states) / TARGETED COMPLETION (specific gates scheduled, W8-02 plan) / EXTENDED PLAN (structured, per outer-window policy).

**For the learner, plainly:** you cannot fail your way out of a career here in one bad morning — every gate has a remediation path and a re-attempt design; what has no path is pretending. The passport shows what's true; graduation makes it official.
