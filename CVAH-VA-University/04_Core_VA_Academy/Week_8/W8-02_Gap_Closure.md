# W8-02 — Competency Gap Closure (As-Needed Track)

**Purpose:** the structured path for learners with unmet gates after the Week-8 cycle — targeted, stigma-free, and never a full-course restart.

**Process:** 1) Gap map: passport + exam analytics + station scores → a one-page list of SPECIFIC deficits ("jugular holder role — airway watch lapses"; "med-math decimal errors under time pressure"), never vague ones ("needs confidence"). 2) Prescription per deficit from the remediation catalog (`23_Remediation/Remediation_Pathways.md`): focused lesson re-take, drill sets, coached repetitions with named counts, or scenario series — each with a completion signal and a re-assessment booking. 3) Cadence: gap-closure learners get priority access to learner-appropriate cases (the training board flags them); progress reviewed weekly by trainer + manager. 4) Outer window: if gates remain unmet as the ~16-week horizon approaches, the Manager/Medical Reviewer conference per `Graduation_Requirements.md` — with honest options on the table.

**Culture clause (posted verbatim in the LMS):** Needing more repetitions is how motor learning works. The record we keep is your current state, not a tally of your tries. The only failure mode this program punishes is faking it.
