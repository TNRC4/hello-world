# TS-ANE-103 — Anesthetic Monitoring Support & Record Keeping

**School/Level:** Anesthesia Academy / VA3-track · **Prereq:** TS-ANE-101/102; ANE-M3/M4 modules · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S1 — the assistant supports the CVT/DVM anesthetist and is never solely responsible for an anesthetized patient; adjusting anesthetic depth/vaporizer is NOT in scope (report; act only on direct in-the-moment instruction) · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**The doctrine: the patient before the monitor.** Every 5-min record cycle begins with hands and eyes: palpebral reflex/jaw tone reported vs baseline trend, MM color, CRT, chest excursion, bag movement, pulse palpation — THEN the numbers: HR/rhythm from ECG, SpO2 + waveform quality, EtCO2 + capnogram shape, BP (method per case), temp, agent %, O2 flow, fluid totals.

**Record keeping:** 5-min interval charting on the anesthesia record; trends drawn, not just digits (the record's slope is the clinical story); every event stamped (drug given by anesthetist, position change, surgeon milestone, alarm); gaps are never backfilled by memory beyond the cycle — a blank beats a guess.

**Report-immediately list (assistant's alarm duty — thresholds per DVM-approved CRC-003 card, not memorized folklore):** any crossing of the case's stated alert bands; any TREND moving steadily toward a band; waveform-number disagreement (SpO2 88% with pristine pleth vs mangled pleth = different urgencies — report both, describe the waveform); capnogram shape change (loss, slant change, baseline lift); bag/chest excursion change; MM/CRT change; temperature drift. Artifact checks the assistant may perform without being asked: probe reposition, lead contact, cuff size/site recheck, line trace — REPORT FIRST or simultaneously, never silently troubleshoot while a real deterioration runs.

**Critical fails:** silent troubleshooting of a deteriorating parameter; backfilled/fabricated record cells; touching vaporizer/APL without direct instruction; leaving patient's head unattended during anesthetist absence moments. **Repetitions:** ≥10 monitored cases as recorder/support with anesthetist sign-per-case; ≥1 staged-drill case. **Practical:** PA-ANES-MON-001. **Knowledge:** QB-ANES monitoring bank + trend cases SIM-ANES-001..006 ≥85%. **Video:** VID-ANE-103. **Sources:** ACVAA small-animal monitoring guidelines; veterinary anesthesia texts; RECOVER (arrest recognition). **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
