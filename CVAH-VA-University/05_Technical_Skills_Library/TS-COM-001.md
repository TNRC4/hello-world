# TS-COM-001 — SBAR-V Abnormal-Finding Report

**School/Level:** Core/VA1 · **Prereq:** TS-ASM-001 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

The University's standard for verbal escalation. SBAR-V = Situation, Background, Assessment-of-observations (NOT diagnosis), Request, Verify.

**Format:** "Dr. K — **S:** Bella Chen, 8 y FS Lab in run 4, post-op spay. **B:** recovered 2 h ago, was comfortable at 14:00. **A:** now HR 148 up from 92, gums pale, CRT 3 s, belly looks fuller to me, she won't settle. **R:** can you come look now? **V:** [confirm the response and next step aloud: 'You're coming now — I'll pull the IV setup.']"

**Rules:** lead with patient + location; give numbers, not adjectives, whenever numbers exist; state trend (what changed from when); "I'm not comfortable with…" is always a legitimate Assessment; urgency word matters — "now," "within the hour," or "FYI at rounds"; close the loop with Verify — an unacknowledged report is not a report. Never diagnose, never propose drug plans; requesting eyes-on is always in scope.

**Common mistakes:** burying the lead ("So she ate at 3, then I noticed…"); adjectives without numbers ("breathing weird"); reporting to another assistant instead of CVT/DVM; no urgency statement; walking away without Verify. **Critical fails (in assessment):** failure to escalate a scripted urgent abnormality; diagnosing/prescribing language. **Assessment:** PA-COM-001 — two scored reports (one live/real, one simulated urgent scenario) using rubric anchors. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-ASM items; scenario items in recognition battery. **Sources:** SBAR literature adapted to veterinary support roles; RECOVER communication principles (closed-loop). **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
