# TS-ECC-102 — CPR Basic Life Support per RECOVER (Compressions & Ventilation Support)

**School/Level:** ECC Academy / VA2+ · **Prereq:** CPR module 100%; TS-EMR-001 ≥8 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S1 (within team codes, role-assigned) · **Revalidation:** 12 months skills refresh (RECOVER cadence)

**Standards (RECOVER 2024 — numeric values live on DVM-approved card CRC-002; educational summary here):** compressions 100–120/min; lateral recumbency default; depth 1/3–1/2 chest width in lateral (dorsal-recumbency technique for select conformations per guidelines); full recoil between compressions (leaning kills output); 2-min uninterrupted cycles, compressor swap each cycle, pauses only at cycle breaks and kept short; conformation-based hand placement: round-chested dogs — highest point of chest; keel-chested — directly over heart; barrel/bulldog conformation — dorsal technique consideration per team lead; cats/small dogs — one-handed circumferential or two-finger over heart per size.

**Ventilation support (assistant-scope):** if not intubated — 30:2 compression:ventilation with tight mask per guidance until airway secured by CVT/DVM; once intubated — continuous compressions with ventilation ~10 breaths/min (avoid hyperventilation — it costs perfusion); assistant may bag per instruction and count aloud; monitoring EtCO2 during CPR reported to lead (trend = compression quality feedback).

**Team behaviors:** closed-loop repeats; compressor announces fatigue early — swap quality beats ego; recorder timestamps cycles/drugs as called; no freelancing.

**Critical fails (drill):** rate/depth persistently out of band uncorrected; leaning (no recoil); >10 s pauses inside cycles; role abandonment. **Repetitions:** manikin/model drills to metronome; quarterly mock codes; annual refresh. **Practical:** PA-ECC-001 (scored 2-min cycle + role scenario). **Knowledge:** QB-ECC (safety-critical). **Sources:** RECOVER 2024 (JVECC; recoverinitiative.org — BLS domain). **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
