# TS-IMG-001 — Radiographic Positioning (Thorax/Abdomen, Lateral & VD)

**School/Level:** Core intro → Imaging Academy depth / VA2 · **Prereq:** Radiation-safety module 100%; dosimetry issued per policy · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** 12 months (radiation safety)

**Radiation safety (non-negotiables):** ALARA — time, distance, shielding; lead apron + thyroid shield always, gloves when hands near beam edge (never IN the primary beam, gloved or not); collimate to the anatomy — collimation is dose control AND image quality; declared-pregnant staff follow policy restrictions; dosimeter worn at collar outside apron; nobody unnecessary in the room; sedation/positioning-aid preference over human hands whenever diagnostic quality allows (sandbags, troughs, tape are teammates).

**Lateral thorax:** right or left lateral per DVM convention; forelimbs pulled cranially clear of the cranial thorax; sternum and spine equidistant from table (rotation check: dorsal spinous processes superimposed); center mid-thorax at caudal scapular border; include thoracic inlet to last rib; **expose at peak inspiration** (watch the chest, fire on the pause after inhale). **VD thorax:** trough; sternum superimposed over spine; forelimbs cranial; inspiration. **Lateral abdomen:** center caudal to last rib; include diaphragm to hip; expose at expiration pause (abdominal detail benefits). **VD abdomen:** same landmarks, expiration.

**Image-quality self-check (the competency):** rotation? motion blur? collimation includes required anatomy? correct marker (R/L) placed BEFORE exposure and correct? exposure adequate per system feedback? The assistant flags TECHNICAL faults and retakes per protocol — never interprets findings. Target: retake rate tracked in analytics; two failed attempts at a view → ask for help, don't burn a third dose.

**Common mistakes:** firing on a moving patient ("almost still" = motion blur = retake = dose); forgetting marker; over-collimating off the diaphragm; hands drifting toward beam during a struggle; skipping the rotation check until after processing. **Critical fails:** any body part in primary beam; no PPE; retaking without changing anything (dose without diagnosis). **Repetitions:** ≥8 coached studies (mix of views/species) with ≤1 retake average over last 4. **Practical:** PA-IMG-001 / station 10. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-IMG + safety items (100% gate). **Video:** VID-IMG-001 (positioning + rotation-check reading). **Sources:** veterinary radiographic positioning atlases (paraphrase); state radiation rules `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: AZ registration/operator requirements]`. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
