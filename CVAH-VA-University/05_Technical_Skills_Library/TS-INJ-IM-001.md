# TS-INJ-IM-001 — Intramuscular Injection

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-INJ-SQ-001 ≥8 · **Scope/Supervision:** P / S1 first 5, then S2; dose verified by CVT/DVM (SCOPE-006) · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**Shared framework:** five-rights, verification, documentation, sharps, and critical fails as TS-INJ-SQ-001.

**Sites (house standards):** *Epaxial* (lumbar, lateral to dorsal spinous processes) — preferred default; *quadriceps* (cranial thigh, angling away from femur) — acceptable, stay cranial and mid-belly of muscle; **the caudal thigh (semimembranosus/semitendinosus) is avoided at CVAH** — sciatic nerve proximity, permanent lameness risk. Small patients: epaxials strongly preferred; volume limits per site per CVT direction (typically ≤0.5–1 mL cats / site-dependent in dogs — confirm on order).

**Procedure deltas:** stabilize muscle between thumb and fingers; 45–90° insertion into muscle belly; **aspirate — mandatory** (IM sites are vascular; IV delivery of an IM-intended drug can be dangerous); inject slowly (muscle stretch hurts); expect more reaction than SQ — restraint plan first (this is why S1 initially); gentle massage after.

**Complications:** pain/vocalization; lameness after thigh injections (report immediately — nerve concern); drug-specific sting (some agents notorious — trainer flags these). **Stop criteria additions:** blood on aspiration → full withdraw, new needle, new site, report if drug already partially delivered. **Critical fails additions:** caudal-thigh site use; skipped aspiration.

**Repetitions:** ≥3 model, ≥5 coached (S1). **Practical:** PA-INJ-SQ-001 with IM anchor set. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-MED IM items (safety-critical). **Video:** VID-INJ-IM-001 (site map graphics). **Sources:** McCurnin's; Plumb's route notes (educational paraphrase only). **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
