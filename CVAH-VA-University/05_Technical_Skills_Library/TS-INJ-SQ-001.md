# TS-INJ-SQ-001 — Subcutaneous Injection

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-MED-001 (five rights) · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2, dose independently verified by CVT/DVM before administration (SCOPE-005) · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**Equipment:** syringe/needle per volume and viscosity (typically 22–25 g); prepared, labeled, verified medication. **Patient considerations:** dehydrated patients absorb SQ meds/fluids slowly; vaccine site conventions matter where hospital follows feline site guidelines `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: CVAH vaccine site map — align with current AAHA/AAFP site recommendations]`.

**Anatomy:** loose subcutis of dorsolateral trunk — "tent" between shoulder blades and caudally; avoid intrascapular midline for feline vaccines per site map.

**Procedure:** 1) Five-rights verification aloud with the CVT/DVM verifier (TS-MED-001). 2) Patient ID check. 3) Tent skin; insert needle through the tent base parallel to the body wall, bevel up. 4) **Aspirate briefly** — blood = withdraw, new needle/site; air whoosh = you're through the far side of the tent, redirect. 5) Inject steadily; withdraw; brief massage unless product label says not to. 6) Watch patient 30 s for immediate reaction; chart immediately (drug, dose, route, site, time, initials).

**Common mistakes:** through-and-through tent injection (wet fur = missed dose — report it, never silently repeat a dose); skipping aspiration; injecting cold vaccine straight from fridge (sting → sensitized patient — warm in hand); recapping. **Complications:** injection-site sting/vocalization (note it); vaccine-associated reactions — facial swelling, hives, vomiting, collapse → IMMEDIATE DVM alert; sterile abscess later (client-reported — record site conventions so it's traceable).

**Stop criteria:** dose discrepancy discovered at any point (STOP, re-verify from source order); patient reaction. **Critical fails:** administering an unverified dose; wrong route (SQ med given via other route or vice versa); silent re-dose after suspected miss; recap by hand. **Repetitions:** ≥3 saline model/practice, ≥5 coached live (mix of vaccines/meds/fluids). **Practical:** PA-INJ-SQ-001. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-MED. **Video:** VID-INJ-SQ-001 (tent mechanics + through-and-through error demo). **Sources:** McCurnin's; product labels; AAHA/AAFP vaccination guidelines (current). **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
