# TS-INP-001 — Inpatient Scheduled Observations & Treatment-Sheet Execution

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-ASM-001 ≥8; TS-MED-PO-001 ≥8 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

**The treatment sheet is a contract:** every cell is either done-and-initialed, or escalated — never blank, never "later." Read the WHOLE sheet at shift start (meds due, monitoring frequency, feeding orders, activity restrictions, DVM alerts thresholds), not one row at a time.

**Scheduled observation bundle (per patient, per frequency ordered):** TPR as ordered; pain observation (house scale posted — score, don't vibe); mentation vs last check (BAR/QAR/lethargic/obtunded — trend matters more than snapshot); appetite/water intake quantified ("ate 1/4 can" not "ate some"); urine/stool/vomit — character and volume noted, I/O math where ordered; fluid line trace + catheter site check (TS-FLU-001 discipline); bandage/drain/incision check per orders (swelling, strike-through, odor, position); bedding dry, patient clean, turning schedule for recumbents; kennel card matches patient matches sheet (ID discipline every single interaction).

**Escalation rules:** any DVM-alert threshold on the sheet → immediate SBAR-V; any NEW abnormality not on the problem list (first vomit, first cough) → report, don't just chart; "patient looks different to me" is a valid escalation — fresh eyes are the point of scheduled checks. Chart the report itself ("Dr. M notified 14:32").

**Handoff (outgoing):** structured verbal per TS-INP-103 pattern: patient, status trend, what's due next hour, what to watch, lines/tubes in place. Incoming assistant walks the ward WITH the sheet before accepting.

**Common mistakes:** batch-charting checks not actually done at charted times (integrity violation, terminable category); feeding a NPO patient (read the WHOLE sheet); silent skipped med because patient "was asleep"; kennel-card drift after cage moves. **Critical fails:** fabricated check; NPO breach; missed sheet-specified alert threshold uncommunicated. **Repetitions:** ≥3 supervised shifts with sheet audit. **Practical:** PA-INP-001 / station 11. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-INP items in ASM bank. **Sources:** hospital treatment-sheet SOP; McCurnin's nursing chapters. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
