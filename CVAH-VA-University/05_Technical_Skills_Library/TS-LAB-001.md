# TS-LAB-001 — Blood Smear Preparation

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-SAM-001 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

**Equipment:** clean slides (frosted-edge), spreader slide, fresh well-mixed EDTA blood, pencil for frosted label.

**Procedure (wedge technique):** 1) One small drop (≈3–4 mm) near frosted end. 2) Spreader slide at ~30–35° BACKED INTO the drop; let blood spread along the back edge by capillary action. 3) Push forward in one smooth, quick, light glide — the drop is pulled, not plowed. 4) Result: thumbprint-shaped smear with a feathered edge ending 1–2 cm before slide end, body with a readable monolayer. 5) Air-dry FAST (wave or fan — slow drying crenates cells); label frosted edge; no coverslip; store flat away from formalin.

**Quality self-check (the competency):** too thick/short smear ← angle too steep or drop too big → lower angle; too thin/runs off end ← angle too shallow/push too slow; streaks/tails ← chipped spreader edge or dirt; holes ← grease/fingerprints. Learner must diagnose their own smear against a visual quality card (LMS INT-11) and remake until acceptable — the analyzer-flag workflow and pathologist both depend on smear quality.

**Critical fails:** submitting an unreadable smear as final without remake; unlabeled slide. **Repetitions:** ≥10 practice smears with the last 3 consecutive meeting quality card; maintained via lab bench work. **Practical:** graduation station 5 element. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-LAB. **Video:** VID-LAB-001 (slow-mo of angle and glide + the four classic bad smears). **Sources:** clinical pathology texts (Cowell's paraphrase); IDEXX smear guidance. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
