# TS-MED-001 — Medication Preparation & Five-Rights Verification

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** W2-03 medication foundations · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2; every dose verified by CVT/DVM before administration · **Revalidation:** 12 months

**The five rights + two:** right **patient**, **drug**, **dose**, **route**, **time** — plus right **documentation** and right **response** (watch and record effect/adverse events). Assistants never select drugs, calculate de novo doses for administration without verification, or modify orders; they prepare *from a written order* and verify.

**Label reading (taught element):** drug name (generic vs brand trap — see LASA below), concentration (mg/mL vs mg/tablet — the single most dangerous line on the label), total volume, expiration, storage, warnings. **Concentration math:** volume (mL) = ordered dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL) — learner must show the arithmetic on the prep label or chart for every liquid draw; a second person checks the math, not just the syringe.

**Vial withdrawal procedure:** verify order → select vial → **first label check** → swab septum → inject air equal to draw volume (vented technique for fragile vials per trainer) → withdraw → expel bubbles → **second label check against syringe label** → label syringe immediately (drug, conc, dose, patient, initials) → **third check at patient-side with verifier**. Ampules: score/snap away from you with gauze, filter-needle awareness where stocked. Never leave an unlabeled syringe anywhere, even "for a second."

**Look-alike/sound-alike (LASA) prevention:** house LASA list posted in pharmacy `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: build from CVAH formulary — classic veterinary pairs to seed: hydroxyzine/hydralazine, ketamine/ketoprofen concentrations, insulin U-40 vs U-100 syringe mismatch]`; TALL-man lettering on shelf labels; never identify a drug by vial shape/color/location.

**Controlled substances:** assistants handle only within the hospital's DEA-registrant procedure — witnessed draws, immediate log entry per TS-PHA-102 workflow, never carried unlogged.

**Error handling:** any discrepancy at any check = STOP, re-verify from the source order; near-misses are REPORTED and logged (blame-free — the system learns); an administered error is reported to DVM immediately, patient monitored, incident logged. Concealing an error is a terminable-category offense per policy and an automatic critical fail forever attached to reassessment.

**Critical fails:** unlabeled syringe leaves your hand; skipped verifier check; concealed error/near-miss; unwitnessed controlled draw. **Repetitions:** ≥10 supervised preps across tablet/liquid/injectable forms with zero unresolved discrepancies. **Practical:** PA-MED-001 (embedded in graduation station 6). **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-MED (math items ≥90% gate for VA2). **Video:** VID-MED-001 (three-check choreography). **Sources:** ISMP medication-safety principles adapted; Plumb's (paraphrase); hospital pharmacy SOP. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
