# TS-MED-PO-001 — Oral, Topical, Ophthalmic & Otic Administration

**School/Level:** Core/VA1–2 · **Prereq:** TS-MED-001 theory · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

**Oral (pilling):** verify per TS-MED-001; dog — open jaw via upper-lip-over-teeth grip, place pill center-base of tongue, close, neutral head, throat stroke/nose puff, confirm swallow (watch for the parked-and-spat pill), chase with water/food where permitted (dry pilling esophagitis risk, esp. cats — doxycycline/clindamycin flagged); cat — pill gun or compounded alternative early, don't lose the patient's trust in three failed manual tries; hiding in food OK per order (verify drug may be given with food); NEVER oral meds to a laterally recumbent, seizing, dyspneic, or non-swallowing patient.

**Liquids:** into cheek pouch, not straight back over the tongue (aspiration); slow, allow swallows.

**Ophthalmic:** clean hands/gloves per product; stabilize hand on patient's head so a jerk moves everything together; drop from ~1 cm without touching tip to eye; ointment ribbon into lower conjunctival sac; multiple eye meds → 5 min apart, order per DVM (verify which first); NEVER apply anything to an eye beyond the written order — eyes deteriorate fast, report changes (increased redness, pain, cloudiness) immediately.

**Otic:** verify eardrum status is addressed in the order (some meds prohibited with ruptured TM — flag if order silent and discharge/pain severe); apply, massage base of ear (the squelch), let patient shake AFTER you look away; note discharge character in record.

**Common mistakes:** charting before confirming swallow; touching dropper tips to surfaces (contaminates whole bottle); wrong eye/ear (AS/AD/AU, OS/OD/OU abbreviations — the approved-abbreviation list is part of this skill's knowledge check); giving with food against label. **Critical fails:** oral med to a contraindicated-state patient; unverified med; wrong eye/ear on a unilateral order. **Repetitions:** ≥5 coached PO (≥2 cats), ≥2 each other route. **Practical:** PA-MED-PO-001 anchors within station 6. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-MED. **Video:** VID-MED-PO-001 (feline pilling correct/incorrect). **Sources:** McCurnin's; product labels. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
