# TS-RES-001 — Lateral Restraint

**School/Level:** Core/VA1 · **Prereq:** TS-HND-001 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

**Equipment:** non-slip mat/table pad; muzzle and towel available. **Patient considerations:** never force brachycephalics, dyspneic, or severely obese patients flat — modified positioning per CVT; painful orthopedic patients need pre-planned side selection.

**Anatomy/physiology:** pressure on the down-side thorax reduces ventilation in compromised patients; neck control protects both handler and patient airway.

**Indications:** procedures requiring recumbency (radiographs, sample collection sites, nail trims, wound care). **Do not proceed:** respiratory distress; suspected spinal injury without DVM direction; escalation beyond your trained level.

**Procedure (canine, from standing):** 1) Announce plan to teammate and patient. 2) Stand at the dog's side; reach over the back; grasp the far-side forelimb (proximal, above carpus) and far-side hindlimb (above hock). 3) Lift far limbs toward you while sliding the dog down your body to the table/floor — controlled, never dropped. 4) Down-side limbs held: forearm across the neck (firm, never on the trachea) with hand holding down-side forelimb; other hand/forearm secures hindquarters holding down-side hindlimb. 5) Constant verbal reassurance; monitor face, gums, breathing throughout. 6) Release: announce, ease pressure, let the patient rise controlled, prevent leaping. **Feline modification:** towel-wrap first for most cats; minimal-hold principle — least restraint that achieves stillness.

**Common mistakes:** forearm pressure on trachea; gripping limbs at joints (injury) instead of above; dropping the patient the last few inches; over-restraining a calm patient into panic; losing hind end while focused on head. **Troubleshooting:** struggling → check for pain source, reduce pressure momentarily, reposition; repeated struggling → stop and reassess plan with CVT.

**Stop criteria:** cyanosis, open-mouth breathing (cat), gasp, collapse, escalating panic, anyone calls "stop." **Escalation:** any stop event; need for a third handler; sedation-candidate patients.

**Cleanup/documentation:** behavior note in record. **Video:** VID-RES-001 (canine takedown correct vs limb-joint grab error). **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-HND. **Practical:** PA-RES-001 §B. **Critical fails:** tracheal pressure; dropping patient; continuing through stop call. **Repetitions:** ≥5 coached canine + ≥3 feline before assessment (variation across sizes builds the motor skill; educational requirement). **Sign-off:** Assessor. **Sources:** McCurnin's restraint chapter; Fear Free handling. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING review.
