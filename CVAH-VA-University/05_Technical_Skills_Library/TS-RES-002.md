# TS-RES-002 — Sternal & Standing Restraint

**School/Level:** Core/VA1 · **Prereq:** TS-HND-001 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

**Equipment:** non-slip surface; towel for cats. **Patient considerations:** sternal is the default for cats and dyspnea-risk patients; standing suits abdominal palpation, temperature, SQ injections in large dogs.

**Procedure (sternal):** 1) Patient sits/lies sternal; one arm under and around the neck directing the head away from the procedure ("hug hold" — cheek-to-cheek risk zone: keep your face out of bite arc). 2) Other arm over the shoulders/back, hand tucking the near forelimb or steadying thorax; lean-in body contact provides most of the security. 3) For cats: "meatloaf" position, forearms bracketing, towel under/around as needed; head control by cupping mandible — never squeezing the throat. **(Standing):** arm under neck as above; second arm under abdomen lifting slightly to prevent sitting; body pressed along patient's side.

**Common mistakes:** throat squeezing; holding so loosely the patient whips around (bite risk to the phlebotomist); face in bite arc; letting hindquarters swing off table. **Troubleshooting:** sitting during standing restraint → knee/arm under abdomen earlier, not force after; head whipping → shorten your hold arc before the next attempt, don't tighten mid-explosion — reset instead.

**Stop criteria/escalation:** identical framework to TS-RES-001. **Critical fails:** throat pressure; face in bite arc after correction; continuing through stop. **Repetitions:** ≥5 coached each posture across ≥2 sizes. **Practical:** PA-RES-001 §C. **Video:** VID-RES-002. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-HND. **Sign-off:** Assessor. **Sources:** McCurnin's; Fear Free. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
