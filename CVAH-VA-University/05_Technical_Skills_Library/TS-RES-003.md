# TS-RES-003 — Venipuncture Positioning (Holder Role: Cephalic, Jugular, Saphenous)

**School/Level:** Core/VA1→VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-RES-001/002 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** none

The holder makes or breaks the draw. This is a distinct skill from restraint: it adds vessel occlusion, limb presentation, and coordination with the phlebotomist.

**Cephalic hold:** patient sternal/sitting; restraint arm around neck as TS-RES-002; occlusion hand cups the elbow from behind, thumb pressing lateral-to-medial across the crook to occlude AND rolling slightly outward to stabilize the vein; limb extended by pushing gently behind the elbow, not pulling the paw. On "release" — thumb off instantly while restraint continues.

**Jugular hold (canine sitting):** patient's rear into your body/corner; one hand cups muzzle from below raising the nose toward ceiling (not hyperextended — airway), other arm hugs shoulders/forelimbs down. **Feline jugular:** patient in sternal at table edge or wrapped, forelimbs held down over edge by second position of same hand, head raised gently. Never over-extend a dyspneic patient's neck.

**Lateral saphenous (canine):** patient in lateral (TS-RES-001); upper hindlimb held above the hock by phlebotomist's helper hand or a third hand; holder occludes by gripping around the distal thigh/proximal tibia. **Medial saphenous (feline):** lateral restraint, upper hindlimb abducted; occlusion pressure in the inguinal region — keep the hold soft, cats resent this position quickly.

**Common mistakes:** occluding too lightly (no vein) or with a squeezing whole-hand grip (patient struggles); pulling the paw instead of pushing the elbow (limb retracts); dropping occlusion late after "release" (hematoma); chatting and missing stress signals — the holder is also the patient monitor. **Stop criteria:** holder is empowered and expected to call stops; feline open-mouth breathing = immediate stop, especially in jugular position.

**Critical fails:** airway compromise in jugular hold; ignoring phlebotomist's "release"; missing overt distress. **Repetitions:** ≥10 coached holds across all three sites before assessment. **Practical:** PA-RES-001 §D. **Video:** VID-RES-003 (all three holds, correct occlusion close-ups). **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-HND/VEN. **Sign-off:** Assessor. **Sources:** McCurnin's. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
