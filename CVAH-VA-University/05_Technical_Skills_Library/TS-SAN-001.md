# TS-SAN-001 — Cleaning, Disinfection & Room Turnover

**School/Level:** Core/VA1 · **Prereq:** none · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** 12 months (infection-control refresher)

**Equipment:** hospital-approved disinfectants `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: products + dilutions + contact times → post on CRC-style card in cleaning closet]`, gloves, paper towels, mop system with clean/dirty separation, laundry bins.

**Key concepts:** cleaning (organic-matter removal) must precede disinfection — disinfectants fail on dirty surfaces; **contact time is the active ingredient**: a sprayed-and-instantly-wiped surface is not disinfected; know which pathogens change the game (parvovirus/panleukopenia and ringworm resist quaternary ammonium products — these require the hospital's designated agent, e.g. accelerated hydrogen peroxide or diluted bleach per protocol, and an isolation-level workflow).

**Procedure (exam-room turnover):** 1) Gloves on; remove gross soil/waste; laundry to bin without hugging it to your scrubs. 2) Clean surfaces top-down (table, scale, counters, door handles, light switches, chairs); 3) Apply disinfectant, walk-away for full labeled contact time — set a timer, do the floor or restock while waiting; 4) Wipe/dry, restock, gloves off, hand hygiene. **Suspected-infectious room:** don PPE per TS-SAN-002, use designated agent, dedicated or disposable equipment, floor last, signage until complete, report to CVT for isolation decision.

**Common mistakes:** spray-and-wipe with zero contact time; same mop water room-to-room; skipping handles/switches; gloves worn out of the room contaminating door handles; topping off spray bottles (dilution drift + contamination). **Stop/escalate:** unknown bodily fluid from suspected-infectious patient → don't freelance, ask; chemical exposure → eyewash + report per OSHA.

**Critical fails:** knowingly skipping contact time on an infectious room; cross-contaminating isolation equipment. **Repetitions:** daily practice; assessed once with an infectious-room scenario. **Practical:** PA-SAN-001 (folded into graduation station 13). **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-SAF. **Video:** none — poster + checklist card instead (laminated, per room). **Sources:** hospital SOP; disinfectant labels; AAHA infection control guidelines. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
