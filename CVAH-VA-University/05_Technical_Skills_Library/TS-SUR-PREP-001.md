# TS-SUR-PREP-001 — Surgical Clip & Initial Skin Prep

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-SAN-001; W5-02 asepsis · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 (patient under DVM/CVT anesthesia care throughout) · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**Equipment:** clippers + sharp #40 blade (dull blades cause clipper burn), vacuum, gauze, chlorhexidine scrub + solution/alcohol per CVAH prep protocol `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: agent sequence, contact cycles]`, non-sterile gloves, positioning aids, warming plan already active.

**Principles:** the prep creates the sterile field's foundation — margins generous per procedure card (typically wide enough for incision extension and draping, per surgeon's posted preferences); clip AFTER induction in the prep area, never in the OR (hair is contamination); prep is aseptic technique even though non-sterile gloves are worn: work incision-out in expanding pattern, never dragging back over cleaned area with the same gauze.

**Procedure:** 1) Confirm patient, procedure, SITE/SIDE against the surgery board and record — **site/side confirmation is verbalized to the anesthetist** ("Bella, left stifle, correct?"); 2) clip with the grain then against for closeness, taut skin, blade flat (edge digs = burn); wide margins per card; 3) vacuum thoroughly; 4) express bladder if ordered (trained separately); 5) initial scrub: gauze with scrub agent, start at incision line, spiral outward, discard, repeat with new gauze until gauze comes away clean AND minimum cycle count/contact time per protocol met; alcohol or solution interleave per protocol; 6) avoid pooling under patient (chemical/thermal injury + grounding-pad issue), avoid soaking drapes-to-be areas; eyes lubricated (anesthetist's task — confirm, don't assume); 7) transport to OR without touching prepped area; final sterile prep in OR per house protocol (may be CVT).

**Common mistakes:** stingy margins; dull-blade burn (report skin lesions found or caused — surgeon decides, concealment is the offense); scrubbing center-out violation; drenching the patient (hypothermia — small patients chill fast); prepping the wrong limb because the board said one thing and the record another (STOP — resolve mismatch before a single hair falls). **Critical fails:** site/side mismatch unresolved before clipping; contaminating prepped site and not re-prepping; unreported clipper laceration. **Repetitions:** ≥5 coached preps across ≥2 procedure types. **Practical:** PA-SUR-PREP-001 / station 9. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-SUR. **Video:** VID-SUR-PREP-001. **Sources:** veterinary surgical nursing texts; AORN-adapted principles; house protocol. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
