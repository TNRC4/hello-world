# TS-VEN-JUG-001 — Jugular Venipuncture

**School/Level:** Core (advanced) / VA3 gate · **Prereq:** TS-VEN-CEPH-001 ≥8; TS-RES-003 jugular hold ≥8 · **Scope/Supervision:** P / S1 until VA3, then S2 · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**Shared framework:** as TS-VEN-CEPH-001 unless noted.

**Why jugular:** larger volumes fast, cleaner samples (less hemolysis), spares peripheral veins for catheters — the standard site for volume draws and many feline panels.

**Anatomy:** jugular groove lateral to trachea, from ramus of mandible to thoracic inlet; occlude LOW (thumb in the groove at thoracic inlet); the carotid lies deep and medial — correct superficial technique and low occlusion keep you away from it.

**Additional contraindications (absolute for assistants — escalate, don't stick):** suspected or known coagulopathy (rodenticide, thrombocytopenia) — jugular hematomas can compress the airway; increased intracranial/intraocular pressure concerns per DVM; severe respiratory distress (positioning risk); neck masses/wounds.

**Procedure deltas:** holder positions per TS-RES-003 (nose up, not hyperextended); occlude at thoracic inlet, watch/palpate groove filling (may take 3–5 s); needle 20–22 g typically, syringe sized to full volume; enter at 25–30° angle, distal-to-proximal (toward the head) or per trainer's taught convention `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: standardize house direction]`; on completion release occlusion → withdraw → firm pressure 60+ s with the neck in neutral position, then recheck before releasing the patient (a missed jugular hematoma is a serious complication).

**Stop criteria additions:** ANY respiratory change in restraint = instant stop and release position; hematoma forming = stop, pressure, report — do not attempt the other side without CVT/DVM approval.

**Critical fails additions:** attempting on a coagulopathy-flagged patient; hyperextending a dyspneic patient's neck; failing to recheck the site before kennel return.

**Repetitions:** ≥3 observed, ≥5 coached successes (≥2 feline) under S1. **Practical:** PA-VEN-CEPH-001 rubric with jugular anchor set. **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-VEN jugular items (safety-critical flagged). **Video:** VID-VEN-JUG-001. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
