# TS-VEN-SAPH-001 — Lateral & Medial Saphenous Venipuncture

**School/Level:** Core/VA2 · **Prereq:** TS-VEN-CEPH-001 ≥8 · **Scope/Supervision:** P/S2 · **Revalidation:** 24 months

**Shared framework:** equipment, physiology, sample handling, sharps, stop criteria (2-attempt limit), escalation, documentation, cleanup, and critical fails are identical to TS-VEN-CEPH-001 unless noted below.

**Anatomy:** *Lateral saphenous* (dog): crosses the lateral surface of the distal tibia obliquely, superficial and mobile — visible in most dogs when occluded at the stifle. *Medial saphenous* (cat): runs along the medial thigh/tibia; large, flat, superficial in cats — often the best feline vessel, but fragile and prone to hematoma.

**Patient considerations:** first choice when forelimbs are catheter-reserved; lateral restraint required, so avoid in dyspneic patients unless CVT approves positioning; the lateral saphenous ROLLS — firm distal stabilization is the entire game; feline medial saphenous bruises easily — smallest workable needle (25 g), gentle aspiration, extended post-draw pressure.

**Procedure deltas vs cephalic:** patient in lateral (TS-RES-001) with holder occluding at stifle (lateral) or applying inguinal pressure (medial); stabilize the vessel with thumb traction distal to site; entry angle shallower (vessel is very superficial — 10–20°); anticipate small volumes and slower flow in cats; on completion, pressure wrap or hold 60–90 s (these sites hematoma more than cephalic).

**Troubleshooting deltas:** vessel rolling → increase distal traction and enter slightly to the side, pinning it against underlying tissue; flow stops when patient flexes leg → holder maintains gentle extension; hematoma starts mid-draw → stop, pressure, move to another site/limb.

**Repetitions:** ≥5 coached successful draws including ≥2 feline medial saphenous. **Practical:** PA-VEN-CEPH-001 administered at a saphenous site (same rubric, site-specific anchors noted). **Knowledge:** QB-CORE-VEN. **Video:** VID-VEN-SAPH-001 (both sites; feline emphasis). **Sources:** as TS-VEN-CEPH-001. **Rev:** 1.0 2026-08-17, PENDING.
