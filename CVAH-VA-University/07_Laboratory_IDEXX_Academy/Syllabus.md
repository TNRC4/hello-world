# Laboratory & IDEXX Academy — Syllabus

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Prereqs: Core Graduate; TS-SAM-001, TS-LAB-001/002 ≥8 · Duration 4–6 weeks · Credential: **Laboratory & IDEXX** · Revalidation 24 months (QC practical)

**Equipment gate:** Module content beyond LID-M1/M2 is generated against the completed Equipment Registry survey (`19_Equipment_Registry/Equipment_Discovery_Survey.md`). `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: installed models — expected family: IDEXX VetLab Station, ProCyte (Dx/One), Catalyst (One/Dx), SediVue Dx, SNAP/SNAP Pro, possibly Vetscan Imagyst]`. For each confirmed analyzer, the Academy builds the 18-point operating unit (purpose → samples → prep → loading → initiation → result workflow → QC → maintenance → cleaning → consumables → warnings → error codes → troubleshooting → contamination prevention → rerun criteria → limitations → manual-verification triggers → escalation) as a hospital-specific job aid derived from — never copying — current manufacturer documentation, with the official manual linked as source of truth.

## Modules
**LID-M1 — Preanalytics Mastery (wk 1).** The error budget: most "lab errors" are sample errors; deep dive on hemolysis/lipemia/icterus effects by analyte class (educational tables, original); collection-to-result timing rules; the artifact catalog with photo library. Extends TS-SAM-001 to specialist depth.
**LID-M2 — Quality Control Doctrine (wk 1).** Why QC before patients; control materials, schedules per analyzer, drift vs shift concepts at working level; QC-failure response tree (rerun control → maintenance step → support call → backup path to reference lab); the maintenance log as legal-grade documentation. Skill: TS-LID-102.
**LID-M3 — Bench & Microscopy (wk 1–2).** Microscope care and use (Köhler-lite setup steps, oil discipline, storage); smear scanning SUPPORT role (monolayer finding, flag-for-review — morphologic interpretation stays with CVT/DVM/pathologist); sediment prep; stain maintenance (precipitate prevention, changing schedules). Skill: TS-LID-103.
**LID-M4 — Analyzer Operations (wks 2–4).** Per-registry-analyzer job-aid modules as above; error-code drill decks built from house history; SNAP-family kit discipline (temperature, timing, reading windows); result-workflow routing in the PIMS. Skill: TS-LID-101 per analyzer.
**LID-M5 — Lab Operations (wk 4+).** Consumable inventory rhythm; reference-lab logistics mastery; turnaround expectations communication; the lab-bench day plan (QC → stats → maintenance windows).

## Credential gates
QB-LABX + per-analyzer item sets ≥85% · QC practical PA-LID-001 (run + document a full QC cycle, respond to a staged failure) · error-code drill ≥90% on house top-10 · case requirement: 4 supervised weeks of bench duty with zero unflagged sample-quality incidents · Medical Reviewer countersign.
