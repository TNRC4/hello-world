# Veterinary Dentistry Support Academy — Syllabus

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Prereqs: Core Graduate; Radiation-safety unit 100% (shared with Imaging); TS-RES-* ≥9 · Duration 5–8 weeks · Credential: **Veterinary Dentistry Support** · Revalidation 24 months

Scope banner: extraction, subgingival work, and diagnosis are DVM (or rule-permitted CVT) territory; polishing/supragingival support per scope row SCOPE-015 policy determination.

## Modules
**DEN-M1 — Oral Anatomy & Disease Literacy (wk 1).** Dental formulae dog/cat; Triadan fluency (INT-D1 drag-label interactive: 20 teeth); crown/root/furcation anatomy; periodontal disease staging concepts; resorptive-lesion and fracture awareness (recognize-and-flag, never diagnose); charting vocabulary (mobility, pockets, furcation grades) for scribing.
**DEN-M2 — The Anesthetized Dental Patient (wk 2).** Cross-credit with Anesthesia M7 recovery unit; dental-specific anesthesia support: long-procedure hypothermia management (this is the coldest table in the building), airway protection vigilance (water + debris + cuffed-tube limits), positioning changes without tube disasters (the "log-roll with the head" drill), feline gag policy, eye protection, prolonged-procedure checklists. Skill: TS-DEN-104.
**DEN-M3 — Dental Radiography (wks 3–4).** Full-mouth series technique; parallel technique (mandibular PM/M) vs bisecting-angle everywhere else; the elongation/foreshortening error pair and their fixes; sensor/plate care; positioning aids; retake discipline; series completeness QA (count before waking). Skill: TS-DEN-101. Practical: full-mouth series on model/cadaver `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: model availability]` then live under supervision.
**DEN-M4 — Instruments & Equipment (wk 5).** Delivery unit maintenance (lines flushed, oil per schedule); handpiece care; scaler tips (ultrasonic power/water settings per unit card); hand-instrument sharpening basics; polisher technique where policy permits (speed, paste, per-tooth dwell limits, why heat matters); pack/sterilization workflow for dental kits. Skills: TS-DEN-102, polishing sub-skill per policy.
**DEN-M5 — Charting Support & Workflow (wk 5+).** Scribing the DVM's calls quickly and correctly onto the dental chart (paper + software per house `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: charting software]`); extraction assistance (instrument sequence anticipation, suction, root-tip vigilance culture — you count and track fragments as told); recovery handoff with dental-specific notes (bleeding watch, food texture orders).

## Credential gates
QB-DENT ≥85% · full-mouth series practical PA-DEN-001 (diagnostic-quality series, ≤20% retakes, complete count) · anesthetized-support practical (staged hypothermia + pooling events caught) · case requirement: 8 dentals supported end-to-end · Medical Reviewer countersign.
