# Diagnostic Imaging Academy — Syllabus

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Prereqs: Core Graduate; radiation safety 100% + dosimetry issued; TS-RES-* ≥9 · Duration 4–6 weeks · Credential: **Diagnostic Imaging** · Revalidation 12 months (radiation-safety refresher)

Scope banner: acquisition and technical quality only; interpretation is diagnosis (DVM).

## Modules
**IMG-M1 — Image Quality Science (wk 1).** Exposure factors at operator level (what kVp/mAs each do to the image, house technique charts as the law); DR/CR workflow per installed system `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: system model → registry]`; the artifact/fault gallery: motion, rotation, under/over-exposure, collimation errors, grid faults, positioning-aid shadows — 20-image "name the fault and the one fix" drill (INT-I1).
**IMG-M2 — Radiation Safety Officer-level Habits (wk 1).** Extends W5-01 to specialty depth: scatter geometry (where the dose actually is in the room), dosimetry program mechanics, pregnancy policy, holding-avoidance systems (aids inventory + sedation advocacy pathway), regulatory registration awareness for AZ `[verify per state radiation control]`.
**IMG-M3 — Core Series Mastery (wks 2–3).** Thorax (3-view where ordered), abdomen, pelvis (incl. OFA-style awareness), spine segments (handling discipline for suspected-spinal patients — DVM-directed only), skull basics; per-view: landmarks, centering, collimation, breathing timing, the rotation self-check; retake-decision trees. Skill: TS-IMG-001→TS-IMG-101.
**IMG-M4 — Orthopedic & Special Views + ECG (wk 4).** Limb series (joint above/below doctrine, flexed/stressed views as directed); horizontal-beam awareness; ECG acquisition (TS-IMG-103): lead placement, artifact prevention, when the strip is "readable" vs redo.
**IMG-M5 — Ultrasound & Advanced Assistance (wks 4–6).** Patient prep (clip maps per study), positioning/comfort during long studies, machine care (probes NEVER dropped/alcohol-soaked — per manufacturer card), procedure assistance (cysto guidance support, FNA support: slide handling chain), room ergonomics/dimming. Skill: TS-IMG-102.
