# Emergency & Critical Care Support Academy — Syllabus

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Prereqs: Core Graduate; TS-EMR-001 ≥8; TPR/restraint ≥9; CPR module 100% · Duration 6–8 weeks · Credential: **Emergency & Critical Care Support** · Revalidation 12 months (CPR skills per RECOVER cadence)

Scope banner: role clarity is the curriculum's spine — assistants recognize, activate, stage, compress, record, run, and support; they do not lead, intubate, or push drugs.

## Modules
**ECC-M1 — Triage & Primary Survey Support (wk 1).** Triage-desk recognition mastery (extends W6-04's set with 40-presentation drill deck INT-E1); the load-and-go doctrine; primary survey A-B-C observation language; triage documentation under pressure; client-in-crisis communication basics (calm scripts, no prognosis ever).
**ECC-M2 — Oxygen, Airway Support & Equipment Deployment (wk 2).** O2 delivery menu (flow-by, mask, cage, nasal-line awareness) with stress-cost accounting; suction mastery; crash-cart anatomy to closed-eyes standard; emergency IV staging drill (<90 s bench standard); rapid diagnostics staging (emergency database concept: PCV/TS, glucose, lactate-if-equipped, smear). Skills: TS-ECC-103.
**ECC-M3 — CPR per RECOVER (wks 3–4).** Full RECOVER-aligned BLS depth (TS-ECC-102 spec is the skills core): conformation-specific compressions, ventilation support, cycles/swaps, EtCO2 feedback, ROSC recognition; ALS awareness level (what the lead is doing and why, so support anticipates: rhythm checks, drug timing rhythm, defibrillation safety choreography — CLEAR discipline); recorder mastery (the log as the code's memory); post-arrest care support awareness. Certification-adjacent note: staff are encouraged toward RECOVER BLS certification where hospital funds `[CVAH-SPECIFIC INPUT REQUIRED: budget decision]` — this academy aligns with but does not replace it.
**ECC-M4 — The Big Presentations (wks 5–6).** Support playbooks (recognition picture → staging list → your loop) for: shock, hemorrhage/trauma, GDV, urethral obstruction, respiratory crisis, seizures/status, heat stroke, anaphylaxis, toxicities (decon support roles only, package doctrine), diabetic crises awareness. Each playbook: original one-page card + drill scenario.
**ECC-M5 — High-Acuity Monitoring & Team Craft (wks 6–8).** Continuous-monitoring patients (the q15 loop and its charting), closed-loop communication drills, treatment-area choreography (who stands where and why), the debrief culture, second-victim awareness (staff wellbeing after bad outcomes — resource pointers).

## Credential gates
QB-ECC + QB-CORE-EMR pool ≥85% (SC 100%) · PA-ECC-001: scored 2-min compression cycle to metronome + role scenario + staging drill under time standard · SIM-CORE-004 lead-support role rotation complete · case requirement: 10 emergency events supported (real or full-mock mix per case availability, ≥4 real) · Medical Reviewer countersign.
