# Inpatient & Hospitalized Patient Care Academy — Syllabus

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Prereqs: Core Graduate; TS-INP-001 ≥8; TS-FLU-001 ≥9; TS-MED-PO-001 ≥9 · Duration 4–6 weeks · Credential: **Inpatient Patient Care** · Revalidation 24 months

Thesis: excellent inpatient care is deliberate observation — the academy trains professional noticing, then wraps logistics around it.

## Modules
**INP-M1 — The Observation Discipline (wk 1).** Trend literacy (delta-thinking over snapshots — formalizes W6-03); quantification habits (I/O math mastery, weight trends as fluid-balance data); pain-scale fluency (house scale deep-dive with video calibration set INT-N1 — inter-rater drills against keyed scores); mentation ladder precision (BAR→QAR→lethargic→obtunded→stuporous language discipline); the "fresh eyes" doctrine institutionalized.
**INP-M2 — Special-Population Playbooks (wks 2–3).** One-page original care cards + drills for: diabetic inpatients (feeding-insulin coupling, hypoglycemia watch signs, the hold-and-report rule); seizure-watch patients (padding, timing, post-ictal handling, cluster escalation); urinary patients (blocked-cat aftercare lines, output math); respiratory patients (O2 cage discipline, handling budgets — every touch costs oxygen); recumbent care mastery (turning, physio-adjacent range-of-motion per orders, bladder/bowel hygiene, pressure-point mapping); infectious/isolation nursing (full barrier workflow, dedicated-everything, the mental model of "the room has a membrane").
**INP-M3 — Lines, Tubes & Devices (wk 3–4).** Specialist-level maintenance observation for: IV lines/multi-lumen awareness, urinary catheters (closed-system discipline, output charting), feeding tubes (NE/E-tube site checks, feeding mechanics per orders, clog prevention/response per protocol), drains (character/volume charting, strike-through rules), oxygen devices, bandage/splint surveillance (the swelling-above/below check, toe-temperature discipline). Skill: TS-INP-101.
**INP-M4 — Nutrition & Elimination (wk 4).** Feeding orders execution (calculated amounts, texture/route, refusal escalation paths); appetite-tempting toolbox within orders; elimination tracking as diagnostics (character scales, photographs-per-policy); litter/walk schedules as data collection.
**INP-M5 — Handoffs & Ward Leadership (wks 5–6).** TS-INP-103 to mastery; ward-round leadership for the routine tier (planning the round, delegating within scope to juniors, the escalation dispatcher role); night/skeleton-shift patterns; family-communication support (approved updates only, the "no news interpretation" rule).

## Credential gates
QB-INPT ≥85% · SIM-INPT-001..003 complete ≥85% · pain-scale inter-rater calibration within 1 point on ≥9/10 video cases · PA-INP-001 (ward practical with planted-abnormality round, device-check circuit, scored handoff) · case requirement: 6 supervised high-dependency shifts incl. ≥1 isolation and ≥1 O2-patient shift · Medical Reviewer countersign.
