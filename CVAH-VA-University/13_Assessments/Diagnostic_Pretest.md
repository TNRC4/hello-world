30 items · unproctored honesty-framed ("guessing well helps nobody") · no pass bar — domain scores route the personal plan; domain ≥90% + practical challenge = module skip eligibility.

Composition: 3 items per Core domain drawn from QB-CORE banks at difficulty 1–2 (item IDs fixed per pretest form to keep norms stable): SAF-001,004,010 · HND-001,003,008 · ASM-001,002,007 · VEN-001,002,010 · IVC-001,002,005 · MED-001,002,011 · FLU-001,002,005 · SUR-001,002,006 · EMR-001,002,004 · LAB-001,002,005.

Routing rules: domain <50% → standard track, extra flagged support in that week; 50–89% → standard track; ≥90% → challenge-out offer (knowledge check ≥90% + direct practical where skill-bound). Prior-experience inventory cross-check: claimed-experienced domains scoring <50% get a kind but explicit calibration conversation — self-assessment accuracy is itself trained here.
