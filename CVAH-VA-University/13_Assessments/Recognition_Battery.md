# Recognition Battery (Graduation Gate 5)

40 items · ≥85% · ALL critical-recognition items (marked ★) correct · media-rich (photos, short video, audio, trend tables) · administered Week 7 after the intensive.

## Domains & counts
Vitals/trends 6 (★2) · catheter/fluid-line findings 6 (★2) · sample artifacts 4 · restraint/behavior escalation 5 (★1) · recovery patterns 5 (★2) · sterile-field events 4 (★1) · emergency presentations 6 (★3) · documentation/med-verification faults 4 (★1).

## Item form
Stimulus (image/video/audio/trend table) → two-part response: (a) what is wrong (MCQ), (b) urgency tier (report-now / report-soon / note-and-monitor). Both parts must be correct for item credit. ★ items are report-now scenarios where a miss would plausibly harm a patient (e.g., GDV silhouette, post-extubation stridor audio, infiltration with pump running, pale-gum recovery photo, male-cat straining video, closed-APL manometer creep [anesthesia-adjacent awareness], pre-initialed future treatment cell).
