# QB-ANES — Perioperative & Anesthesia Academy (30 items)

### ANES-001 | ANE-M1 | D2 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Cardiac output equals:
A) HR × blood pressure B) HR × stroke volume C) Preload × afterload D) SpO2 × HR
KEY: B — Stroke volume itself shaped by preload, afterload, contractility.

### ANES-002 | ANE-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Most inhalant anesthetics affect the cardiovascular system by:
A) Raising BP B) Dose-dependent vasodilation and depression → hypotension risk deepens with depth C) No effect D) Increasing contractility
KEY: B — Why depth and BP are read together.

### ANES-003 | ANE-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: EtCO2 suddenly reads near-zero with a flat capnogram mid-case. Most urgent possibilities:
A) Calibration drift only B) Disconnection, tube dislodgement/obstruction, or arrest — check patient and circuit NOW C) Normal variation D) Absorber exhausted
KEY: B — Flat-line capnograph is a hands-on-patient moment. [DIST: D raises baseline, not flatline.]

### ANES-004 | ANE-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:N

### ANES-005 | ANE-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
