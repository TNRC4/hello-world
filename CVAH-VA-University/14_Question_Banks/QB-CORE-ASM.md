# QB-CORE-ASM — Patient Assessment, Inpatient, Communication (20 items)

### ASM-001 | TS-ASM-001 | D1 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Respiratory rate is counted:
A) After handling, for accuracy B) Before handling, from a distance C) During restraint D) Only if the patient seems sick
KEY: B — Handling inflates the number.

### ASM-002 | TS-ASM-001 | D1 | Recall | SC:N
Q: One respiration equals:
A) Each chest movement B) One full inhale + exhale cycle C) Two cycles D) One minute of breathing
KEY: B — Full cycle in + out. [DIST: A is the classic doubling error.]

### ASM-003 | TS-ASM-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: A pulse deficit is:
A) Slow pulse B) A heartbeat heard without a corresponding femoral pulse C) Weak pulse D) Missing thermometer
KEY: B — Report it; beats aren't perfusing.

### ASM-004 | TS-ASM-002 | D1 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Normal CRT is:
A) <2 seconds B) 4–5 seconds C) 10 seconds D) Instant only
KEY: A.

### ASM-005 | TS-ASM-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: 9 AM temp reads 105.2°F on a hospitalized dog. You:
A) Chart it for rounds B) Re-take at noon C) Report now per out-of-band rule D) Cool the patient yourself
KEY: C — Out-of-band = report at discovery. [DIST: D — cooling is a treatment decision.]

### ASM-006 | TS-ASM-004 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Best charting of a pain observation:
A) "Seems painful" B) "Painful abdomen — pancreatitis?" C) "Tensed and vocalized on abdominal touch; pain score 3/4 per house scale" D) "Uncomfortable"
KEY: C — Observable + scaled; B diagnoses.

### ASM-007 | TS-COM-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: The V in SBAR-V requires you to:
A) Validate your feelings B) Verify/close the loop on the response and next step aloud C) Volunteer for more work D) Video the patient
KEY: B — An unacknowledged report is not a report.

### ASM-008 | TS-INP-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Treatment-sheet cell you couldn't complete (patient at imaging). Correct handling:
A) Leave blank silently B) Initial it anyway C) Escalate/annotate per protocol so it's visibly owned D) Cross it out
