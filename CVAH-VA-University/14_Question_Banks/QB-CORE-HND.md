### HND-001 | TS-HND-001 | D1 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Early canine appeasement/stress signals include:
A) Wagging and play bow B) Lip licking, yawning, looking away C) Barking only D) Rolling over always means belly rub
KEY: B — The early ladder rungs you must name on sight.

### HND-002 | TS-HND-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: A cat in the carrier is pupil-blown, ears flat, silent. Best opening move:
A) Reach in confidently B) Dump the carrier C) Remove the carrier top calmly with towel ready, work in the bottom half D) Shake treats
KEY: C — Disassembly beats extraction; silence + dilated pupils = pre-explosion.

### HND-003 | TS-RES-001 | D1 | Recall | SC:Y
Q: In lateral restraint your forearm across the neck must never:
A) Touch fur B) Press on the trachea C) Hold the down forelimb D) Stay still
KEY: B — Airway is untouchable.

### HND-004 | TS-RES-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Mid-restraint, a cat begins open-mouth breathing. You:
A) Finish quickly B) Restrain tighter C) Stop and release position immediately, alert CVT D) Offer water
