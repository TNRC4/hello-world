# QB-CORE-LAB — Lab Bench (12 items)
Rewritten to Assessment_Item_Quality_Standard 2026-08.

### LAB-001 | TS-LAB-001 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: The spreader slide is:
A) Backed into the drop at ~30–35°, then pushed in one smooth glide B) Pressed onto the drop at ~60° and drawn back slowly C) Laid flat on the drop and rotated D) Pushed into the drop from the far end at any angle
KEY: A. B, C, D each produce a recognizable bad smear (short/thick, smashed, streaked).

### LAB-002 | TS-LAB-001 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Slow-drying a smear causes:
A) Overstaining at the feathered edge B) A thicker monolayer C) Nothing — drying speed is cosmetic D) Cell crenation artifacts
KEY: D — wave or fan it dry. A and B are invented mechanisms; C is the shrug this item exists to remove.

### LAB-003 | TS-LAB-002 | D2 | L3 | SC:N
Q: Paired hematocrit tubes read 34% and 41%. You:
A) Report the average, 37.5% B) Report 41% — the higher spin was more complete C) Redo — disagreement beyond 2% means technique or reading error D) Report 34% — the lower tube had less trapped plasma
KEY: C. A, B, D each apply confident-sounding statistics to data that just failed its own quality check.

### LAB-004 | TS-SAM-002 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Urine analyzed 4 hours after sitting at room temperature will show:
A) The same results, just slightly more concentrated B) Crystal and cell changes — the delay corrupts the data C) Only a pH drift, which the analyzer corrects for D) A falsely low glucose and nothing else of note
KEY: B — analyze under an hour or refrigerate and note it. A, C, D each admit a small distortion to hide the general one.

### LAB-005 | TS-LAB-003 | D1 | L1 | SC:N
Q: Centrifuge loads are:
A) Balanced opposite each other, lid secured B) Grouped on one side for easy retrieval C) Fine unbalanced below half speed D) Run lidless briefly for quick spins
KEY: A. B and C wobble the rotor toward failure; D is an aerosol and shrapnel policy violation at any speed.

### LAB-006 | TS-SAM-003 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Cytology from both ears is collected as:
A) One swab rolled on two slide ends B) Separate labeled swabs and slides per ear at collection C) Two swabs, labeled at the bench afterward D) One slide, left ear on top half
KEY: B. A and D merge sides that may need different treatment; C labels after the moment memory can swap them.

### LAB-007 | TS-SAM-001 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Serum separation timing matters because:
A) Serum volume evaporates measurably within a few hours B) The separator gel barrier weakens as the tube sits C) Prolonged cell contact shifts glucose, potassium, and others D) Reference labs reject tubes spun outside their windows
KEY: C — separate within the directory's window. A, B, D are lab-flavored trivia.

### LAB-008 | TS-LAB-002 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Lipemic plasma affects the refractometer TS reading by:
A) Falsely raising it — flag lipemia with the result B) Falsely lowering it as the light scatters away C) Blocking any usable reading until the sample is cleared D) Nothing measurable — refractometers read through haze
KEY: A. B inverts it; C overstates it; D understates it — the flag is the competency.

### LAB-009 | TS-SAM-002 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Fecal antigen testing wants a sample that is:
A) Any age if refrigerated at some point B) Dried for easier handling C) Fresh, adequate volume, chilled if delayed per directory D) Mixed from several days for representativeness
KEY: C. A, B, D each trade sample integrity for convenience.

### LAB-010 | TS-LAB-001 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Holes scattered through your smear usually mean:
A) The blood was too old B) Grease or fingerprints on the slide C) The drop was too small D) The stain was exhausted
KEY: B — handle by edges, use clean slides. A, C, D produce other artifacts, not holes.

### LAB-011 | TS-SAM-004 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: A complete reference-lab submission requires:
A) Correct tubes, labels matching the requisition, right temperature, courier timing B) The labeled tubes plus a handwritten note with the patient's name C) Whatever packaging and paperwork the courier accepts that day D) The tubes plus a printed copy of the full medical record
KEY: A. B is the partial version that generates callbacks; C outsources standards; D overshares and still misses the requisition match.

### LAB-012 | TS-LAB-003 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y
Q: A tube shatters inside the running centrifuge. You:
A) Stop the rotor and open immediately to assess the damage B) Keep the lid closed briefly for aerosols, then gloves-and-forceps cleanup, log it C) Run a cleaning cycle to sanitize before touching fragments D) Tip the cooled fragments into the regular trash carefully
KEY: B. A opens into the aerosol cloud; C grinds glass through the rotor; D puts sharps in soft trash.
