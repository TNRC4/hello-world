# QB-CORE-SAF — Safety, Infection Control, Scope (20 items)

### SAF-001 | W0-02 | D1 | Recall | SC:Y
Q: When is it acceptable to recap a used needle by hand?
A) When the sharps container is far away B) When the needle is clean C) Never D) When wearing gloves
KEY: C — One-hand/no-recap doctrine; recapping is the classic self-stick mechanism.

### SAF-002 | W0-02 | D1 | Recall | SC:Y
Q: First action after any bite or scratch?
A) Finish the procedure B) Immediate thorough wash with soap and running water C) Apply bandage and continue D) Photograph the wound
KEY: B — Then report same shift and document. [DIST: A is the real-world norm being untrained.]

### SAF-003 | TS-SAN-001 | D1 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Disinfectants reliably work only when applied to…
A) Any surface quickly B) A pre-cleaned surface for the full labeled contact time C) A wet surface D) A surface twice
KEY: B — Organic matter inactivates; contact time is the active ingredient.

### SAF-004 | W0-03 | D1 | Recall | SC:Y
Q: Which tasks does Arizona law reserve absolutely from veterinary assistants?
A) Injections and radiographs B) Diagnosis, prognosis, prescription, surgery C) Restraint and TPR D) Lab tests and bandaging

### SAF-005 | TS-SAN-001 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: A parvo-suspect just left exam 2. Which product class is the WRONG choice for the room?
A) Standard quaternary ammonium disinfectant B) Accelerated hydrogen peroxide per protocol C) Properly diluted bleach per protocol D) The hospital's designated parvocidal agent

### SAF-006 | TS-SAN-002 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Correct PPE DOFFING starts with:
A) Gown B) Mask C) Gloves, then hand hygiene D) Eye protection
KEY: C — Gloves are dirtiest; hygiene between steps prevents self-contamination.

### SAF-007 | W0-02 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: A coworker drops a used needle on the floor mid-restraint. Best action?
A) Kick it aside until done B) Pause safely, retrieve with forceps/one-hand scoop to sharps, then continue C) Pick it up quickly by the sharp end D) Leave it for cleaning staff
