### SAF-001 | W0-02 | D1 | L1 | SC:Y
Q: Recapping a used needle by hand is acceptable:
A) When the sharps container is across the room B) Never C) When wearing double gloves D) When the needle was used on a clean draw
KEY: B — the absolute is the taught rule; each other option is a real rationalization heard on floors.

### SAF-002 | W0-02 | D1 | L2 | SC:Y
Q: Immediately after a cat scratch you should:
A) Wash thoroughly with soap and running water B) Apply pressure and a bandage C) Finish the restraint task, then clean up D) Photograph it for the incident report
KEY: A — minutes of washing, then same-shift report. B skips decontamination; C is the real-world norm being untrained; D documents before decontaminating.

### SAF-003 | TS-SAN-001 | D1 | L2 | SC:N
Q: A disinfectant reliably works only on a surface that is:
A) Rinsed first and still visibly wet B) Sprayed twice from close range C) Pre-cleaned, with full labeled contact time D) Warmed to room temperature or above
KEY: C. A, B, D each swap ritual for the two things that matter: organic-matter removal and time.

### SAF-004 | W0-03 | D1 | L1 | SC:Y
Q: Arizona law absolutely reserves which set from veterinary assistants?
A) Injections, radiographs, lab tests B) Restraint, TPR, dental polishing C) Anesthesia monitoring and recovery D) Diagnosis, prognosis, prescription, surgery
KEY: D — A.R.S. § 32-2211(5). A and C list delegable tasks; B mixes delegable with policy-dependent.

### SAF-005 | TS-SAN-001 | D2 | L3 | SC:N
Q: After a parvo-suspect leaves exam 2, which product choice fails?
A) A standard quaternary ammonium spray B) Diluted bleach per protocol C) Accelerated hydrogen peroxide per protocol D) The hospital's designated parvocidal agent
KEY: A — non-enveloped viruses shrug off quats; the other three are the correct family.

### SAF-006 | TS-SAN-002 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: PPE doffing begins with:
A) The gown, to free your arms B) The mask, to breathe freely C) Gloves, then hand hygiene D) Eye protection
KEY: C — gloves are the dirtiest item; hygiene between steps prevents self-contamination.

### SAF-007 | W0-02 | D2 | L3 | SC:N
Q: A used needle lands on the floor mid-restraint. Best handling:
A) Slide it aside with your shoe until the patient is done B) Pause safely and move it to sharps with a one-hand scoop or forceps C) Ask the owner to step back and continue D) Note its location and collect it at cleanup
KEY: B — control the hazard as soon as safely possible; A, C, D all leave a live sharp in a traffic zone.

### SAF-008 | W0-03 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y
Q: Cage card and open record disagree on the patient's name before an injection. You:
A) Trust the cage card — it travels with the patient B) Trust the record — it's the legal document C) Give the med and flag the mismatch after D) Stop everything until identity is resolved
KEY: D. A and B each pick a side of a conflict that must be RESOLVED, not adjudicated by rank of paperwork; C reverses the order of operations fatally.

### SAF-009 | W0-02 | D1 | L2 | SC:N
Q: You smell anesthetic gas near the machine mid-case. You:
A) Report it to the anesthetist immediately B) Open a window and increase room airflow C) Check whether the smell fades in a minute D) Turn the vaporizer down slightly

### SAF-010 | W5-01 | D1 | L1 | SC:Y

### SAF-011 | W5-01 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y

KEY: C — lead attenuates but does not make the primary beam safe; A, B, D are the standard rationalizations.

### SAF-014 | W0-03 | D2 | L3 | SC:N

### SAF-020 | W6-04 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y
