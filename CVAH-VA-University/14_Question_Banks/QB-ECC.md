# QB-ECC — Emergency & Critical Care Academy (15 items; extends QB-CORE-EMR)

### ECC-001 | ECC-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Primary survey order:
A) Temperature first B) Airway → Breathing → Circulation observations, reported as found C) Weight first D) Paperwork
KEY: B — Your observations feed the DVM's survey; obvious failures called out instantly.

### ECC-002 | ECC-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Oxygen delivery with least patient stress for a fractious dyspneic cat:
A) Tight mask B) Flow-by/oxygen cage per direction — stress kills dyspneic cats C) Intubation by you D) None until DVM arrives
KEY: B.

### ECC-003 | TS-ECC-102 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Compressor position for a barrel-chested bulldog per RECOVER conformation guidance:
A) Standard lateral always B) Dorsal recumbency technique consideration per team lead C) Sternal D) Never compress bulldogs
KEY: B — Conformation-specific technique is a 2024 emphasis.

### ECC-004 | ECC-M3 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: During CPR, EtCO2 suddenly jumps markedly higher. Meaning:
A) Bad news B) Possible ROSC (return of spontaneous circulation) — announce to lead immediately C) Broken monitor D) Stop compressions to admire
KEY: B — Announce; the LEAD decides rhythm/pulse checks at cycle break.

### ECC-005 | ECC-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Emergency IV setup staging means:
A) Placing the catheter yourself in a crashing patient B) Clipper, prep, catheters ×2 sizes, T-port primed, flush, tape torn, fluids spiked — laid out for the placer C) Waiting for orders D) One catheter only
KEY: B — Staging is assistant gold; placement in crashing patients is CVT/DVM.

### ECC-006 | ECC-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Triage priority among simultaneous arrivals: (1) laceration walking, (2) open-mouth cat, (3) vomiting puppy bright and wiggly:
A) 1,2,3 B) 2 first — airway/breathing outranks all; then DVM directs C) 3,2,1 D) First-come order
KEY: B.

### ECC-007 | ECC-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Heat-stroke arrival cooling by assistants:
A) Ice bath immediately B) Per DVM direction — typically active cooling with tepid water/fan, stopping per target; ice can worsen via vasoconstriction/shivering C) None D) Alcohol bath
KEY: B — You stage and execute per direction, know the rationale.

### ECC-008 | ECC-M3 | D2 | Recall | SC:Y
Q: Intubated CPR ventilation target:
A) 30 breaths/min B) ~10 breaths/min, avoiding hyperventilation C) 2 per cycle D) Continuous bagging
KEY: B — Hyperventilation costs venous return/perfusion (RECOVER).

### ECC-009 | ECC-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Rodenticide-exposure dog arrives stable. Sample staging you can anticipate:
A) None B) Blood draw setup incl. citrate tube (coag testing likely) — with extra care at puncture sites and pressure C) Urine only D) Fecal
KEY: B — Anticipation with coagulopathy-aware technique.
