# QB-IMG — Diagnostic Imaging Academy (12 items; extends QB-CORE SAF/IMG items)

### IMG-001 | IMG-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Rotation on a lateral thorax is detected by:
A) Gut feeling B) Dorsal spinous processes not superimposed / rib heads doubled C) Exposure index D) Collimation
KEY: B — The self-check before anyone walks away.

### IMG-002 | IMG-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Thorax exposure timing:
A) Any moment B) Peak inspiration — watch the chest, fire on the pause C) Expiration D) Mid-pant
KEY: B — Expiration favors abdomen instead.

### IMG-003 | IMG-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: A colleague reaches bare-handed to steady a paw "just at the edge" during exposure. This is:
A) Efficient B) A stop-and-speak event — nothing enters the beam; aids/tape/sandbags exist C) Fine with a badge D) Fine briefly
KEY: B.

### IMG-004 | IMG-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Markers (R/L) are placed:
A) In post-processing when possible B) Physically before exposure, correct side, in collimated field but off anatomy C) Never needed digitally D) By the DVM
KEY: B — Digital relabeling invites laterality errors.

### IMG-005 | IMG-M3 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Retake discipline means:
A) Shoot until pretty B) Identify the specific fault, change that one thing, retake once; two failures = get help C) Never retake D) Blame the machine
KEY: B — Dose without diagnosis is the sin.

### IMG-008 | IMG-M5 | D2 | Apply | SC:N

### IMG-010 | IMG-M1 | D3 | Analyze | SC:N
Q: VD abdomen: image grainy/noisy, patient panting through it, exposure per chart. Priority fix for retake:
A) More mAs blindly B) Motion control first — brief pause timing/aids/sedation conversation; noise vs blur distinguished before touching technique C) New machine D) Skip the view
