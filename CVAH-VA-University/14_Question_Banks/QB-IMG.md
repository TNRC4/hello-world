# QB-IMG — Diagnostic Imaging Academy (12 items; extends QB-CORE SAF/IMG items)
Rewritten to Assessment_Item_Quality_Standard 2026-08.

### IMG-001 | IMG-M1 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Rotation on a lateral thorax is confirmed by:
A) The cardiac silhouette looking enlarged B) Rib heads doubled and spinous processes off-midline C) The diaphragm appearing blurred D) Collimation cutting the sternum
KEY: B — the self-check before anyone walks away. A, C, D are real image features with other causes.

### IMG-002 | IMG-M1 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Thoracic exposures are timed to:
A) Peak inspiration — fire on the pause after inhale B) End expiration for stillness C) Mid-breath for average lung volume D) Whenever panting briefly stops
KEY: A. B is the ABDOMEN's timing (the paired fact); C and D institutionalize motion.

### IMG-003 | IMG-M2 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y
Q: A colleague's bare fingers steady a paw "just at the collimator's edge" as they fire. You:
A) Let it pass — the edge dose is negligible B) Mention it after the study so the retake isn't rushed C) Stop-and-speak now — nothing enters the beam; aids exist D) Offer to hold instead with your lead gloves on
KEY: C. A and B trade dose for politeness; D substitutes a better-shielded violation for the violation.

### IMG-004 | IMG-M1 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: R/L markers are:
A) Added digitally in post-processing when missed B) Physically placed before exposure, off the anatomy C) Optional on obvious laterals D) The DVM's responsibility at reading
KEY: B — digital relabeling invites laterality errors; A is the workflow that causes them; C and D relocate the duty.

### IMG-005 | IMG-M3 | D2 | L3 | SC:N
Q: Retake discipline means:
A) Never retaking — the DVM reads what the first attempt produced B) Retaking as many times as the image quality requires C) Naming the fault, changing that one thing, retaking once; two failures = help D) Stepping the exposure upward with each successive attempt
KEY: C. A abandons quality; B and D burn dose without a differential diagnosis of the fault.

### IMG-006 | IMG-M2 | D2 | L1 | SC:Y
Q: Your dosimeter is worn:
A) At collar level outside the apron, by you only B) Under the apron at chest level C) Clipped to the machine console D) Shared with whoever is in the room that day
KEY: A. B measures what the apron blocked; C measures the room; D measures nobody.

### IMG-007 | IMG-M4 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: For an orthopedic limb study, the joint of interest is:
A) Placed at the cassette edge so the whole limb fits in frame B) Centered, beam through the joint space, adjacent joint included per protocol C) Positioned wherever the patient's comfort allows that day D) Captured in one wide survey view along with everything else
KEY: B. A distorts exactly what matters most; C makes tolerance the technique; D trades diagnostic geometry for coverage.

### IMG-008 | IMG-M5 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Abdominal ultrasound prep involves clipping because:
A) Hair harbors bacteria near the probe B) Gel spreads more evenly on skin C) Hair traps air, which destroys acoustic contact D) It marks the study area for repeat exams
KEY: C — air is the enemy of the image; A, B, D are grooming logic.

### IMG-009 | IMG-M4 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: ECG artifact prevention starts with:
A) Shutting off the fluorescent lights nearest the table B) Contact quality — placement, gel/alcohol, a calm patient C) Printing the strip at a slower paper speed D) Setting the machine's artifact filtering to maximum
KEY: B. A and D fight interference after inviting it; C hides tremor instead of preventing it.

### IMG-010 | IMG-M1 | D3 | L4 | SC:N
Q: VD abdomen: image unsharp; the patient panted through the exposure; technique matched the chart. The retake priority:
A) Raise the mAs so the shorter exposure overpowers the blur B) Motion control — timing, aids, or a sedation conversation — before touching technique C) Switch to a faster receptor setting and tighter collimation D) Accept the image — panting patients rarely image better than this
KEY: B — diagnose the artifact class first; A and C adjust physics against a moving target; D surrenders a solvable problem.

### IMG-011 | IMG-M2 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: A declared-pregnant team member's imaging duties:
A) Continue unchanged, with doubled lead as the extra margin B) Follow the declared-pregnancy policy pathway — typically no exposure-room duties C) Become her own informed decision about acceptable personal risk D) Shift to positioning and holding duties only, never firing exposures
KEY: B. A and D improvise protections; C ignores that policy, not preference, governs occupational dose.

### IMG-012 | IMG-M3 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: The retake-rate metric exists to:
A) Rank imaging staff for scheduling priority B) Justify equipment purchases C) Surface positioning-training needs and equipment faults D) Discourage retakes even when needed
KEY: C — dose and workflow both improve when it falls for the right reasons; A and D weaponize it; B occasionally borrows it.
