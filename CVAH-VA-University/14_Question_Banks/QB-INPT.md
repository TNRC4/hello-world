# QB-INPT — Inpatient Care Academy (12 items)

### INPT-001 | INP-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Recumbent patients are repositioned:
A) Daily B) On a turning schedule (typically q4h or per orders) with skin checks — atelectasis and pressure injury prevention C) When they complain D) Never — rest is healing
KEY: B.

### INPT-002 | INP-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Early pressure-injury warning:
A) Open wound only B) Persistent redness/thinning fur over bony prominences — pad, reposition, report C) Limping D) Nothing visible ever
KEY: B.

### INPT-003 | INP-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Diabetic inpatient refuses breakfast before scheduled insulin. You:
A) Give insulin anyway per sheet B) Hold and report BEFORE the dose — feeding status changes insulin decisions C) Double food at lunch D) Skip silently
KEY: B — The sheet assumed eating; assumptions get escalated.

### INPT-004 | INP-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Post-seizure (post-ictal) patients are:
A) Back to normal instantly B) Often disoriented — low-stimulation environment, padded space, close observation, events timed/logged C) Hungry only D) Contagious
KEY: B.

### INPT-005 | INP-M3 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Oxygen-cage patients and door discipline:
A) Open freely B) Every opening dumps the O2 environment — plan tasks to batch, use ports where fitted, watch tolerance during any opening C) Doors stay locked D) No checks needed
KEY: B.

### INPT-006 | INP-M1 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: "Ate some" on an inpatient chart is:
A) Fine B) Non-data — quantify ("1/4 can of X at 14:00") for appetite trending C) Optimistic D) Required phrasing
KEY: B.

### INPT-007 | INP-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y
Q: Isolation-ward workflow direction is:
A) Isolation first, fresh energy B) Isolation LAST; dedicated PPE/equipment; nothing walks back out un-disinfected C) Alternating D) Whoever's free
KEY: B.

### INPT-008 | INP-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: Urinary catheter collection systems are kept:
A) On the cage floor above bladder level sometimes B) Closed, below patient level, line untangled, output measured/charted per schedule C) Open for airflow D) Emptied weekly
KEY: B.

### INPT-009 | INP-M3 | D3 | Analyze | SC:Y
Q: Respiratory-watch patient: RR 32→40→48 across checks, effort up "slightly," still eating. You:
A) Recheck in 4 h — appetite is good B) Report the trend now — trajectory outranks the reassuring appetite C) Note for rounds D) Walk the patient to test
KEY: B.

### INPT-010 | INP-M1 | D2 | Recall | SC:N
Q: I/O tracking totals:
A) Vibes B) Measured ins (fluids, water drunk quantified) vs outs (urine weights/estimates flagged, vomit/stool events) per schedule C) Fluids only D) Once daily always
KEY: B.

### INPT-011 | INP-M4 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: A bandage smells odd at scheduled check; strike-through absent. You:
A) Re-wrap over it B) Report — odor is a bandage red flag (with wetness, slippage, swelling above/below, patient chewing) C) Perfume it D) Note tomorrow
KEY: B.

### INPT-012 | INP-M5 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
Q: The incoming shift accepts the ward when:
A) The clock strikes B) After walking the ward with the sheet, hearing per-patient trends/watch-fors, and reading back the critical items C) Keys hand over D) The group chat updates
