### INPT-001 | INP-M1 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Recumbent patients are repositioned:
A) On a schedule — typically q4h or per orders — with skin checks B) Whenever they vocalize or shift as if uncomfortable C) Once per shift, at handoff, with both assistants lifting D) At the first sign of a pressure mark over a bony point
KEY: A. B, C, D wait for the injury the schedule exists to prevent.

### INPT-002 | INP-M1 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: The earliest pressure-injury warning is:
A) An open, draining sore over the point of the hip B) The patient repeatedly refusing to lie on one particular side C) Persistent redness or fur thinning over bony points D) Dependent swelling along the down-side lower limbs
KEY: C — pad, reposition, report at THIS stage. A is the late stage; B and D are real findings with other meanings.

### INPT-003 | INP-M2 | D2 | L3 | SC:Y
Q: The diabetic inpatient refuses breakfast; insulin is due on the sheet. You:
A) Give the insulin as written — the treatment sheet is the order B) Give half the scheduled dose as a reasonable compromise C) Offer a tastier food and dose if any amount gets eaten D) Hold and report before the dose — feeding status changes insulin decisions
KEY: D. A follows the sheet off a cliff; B prescribes; C improvises a threshold the DVM never set.

### INPT-004 | INP-M2 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: A post-ictal patient is best kept:
A) With normal ward traffic for stimulation B) In a padded, low-stimulation space, observed, events timed C) With food available to restore energy D) Sedated per the standing seizure protocol
KEY: B. A and C misjudge the disoriented brain and the aspiration risk; D invents an order.

### INPT-005 | INP-M3 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: Oxygen-cage door discipline means:
A) Batching tasks and using ports — every opening dumps the environment B) Timing every opening to the patient's calmest periods C) Restricting cage openings to one designated team member D) Holding each individual opening to under thirty seconds
KEY: A. B, C, D each ritualize openings instead of minimizing them.

### INPT-006 | INP-M1 | D2 | L1 | SC:N
Q: The charting standard for appetite:
A) "Ate some" with your initials B) "Good appetite" if the bowl is emptier C) "Ate 1/4 can of i/d at 14:00" D) A photo of the bowl in the record
KEY: C — quantified intake trends; A and B are non-data; D is data without a number.

### INPT-007 | INP-M4 | D2 | L2 | SC:Y
Q: Isolation nursing traffic runs:
A) Isolation first, while your PPE is freshest and cleanest B) Isolation last; dedicated equipment; nothing walks out un-disinfected C) Alternating isolation and clean patients to balance the load D) Assigned to whoever carries the lightest patient list
KEY: B. A is the seductive fresh-PPE logic; C and D describe fair workload and terrible epidemiology.

### INPT-008 | INP-M2 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: A urinary collection system is maintained:
A) Open to air for drainage, below the patient B) Closed, above bladder level so gravity assists flushing C) Closed, below patient level, output measured on schedule D) Clamped between measurements to preserve samples
KEY: C. A invites ascending infection; B reverses the gravity that makes it work; D dams urine against the bladder.

### INPT-009 | INP-M3 | D3 | L4 | SC:Y
Q: Respiratory-watch patient: RR 32 → 40 → 48 across three checks, effort "slightly" up, still eating well. You:
A) Recheck in two hours — appetite argues against crisis B) Report the trend now — trajectory outranks the reassuring appetite C) Move the patient nearer the oxygen cage as a precaution D) Note the trend prominently for handoff
KEY: B. A and D let one comforting data point outvote three worsening ones; C stages equipment around an unreported problem.

### INPT-010 | INP-M1 | D2 | L1 | SC:N
Q: I/O tracking totals are built from:
A) The fluid-pump's delivered totals across the full day B) Measured ins and outs — quantified drinking, weighed litter/pads, flagged estimates C) The patient's weight change, which integrates everything anyway D) Structured morning and evening impressions from the ward team
KEY: B. A, C, D each capture one shadow of the balance the math needs.

### INPT-011 | INP-M4 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: At a scheduled bandage check you notice an odd smell; everything else looks fine. You:
A) Report it — odor is a red flag even without visible changes B) Mark it for recheck at the next scheduled round C) Reinforce the wrap so contamination can't enter D) Swab under the edge for a culture in case
KEY: A. B schedules a warning sign; C seals in whatever is producing the smell; D initiates diagnostics that aren't yours to start.

### INPT-012 | INP-M5 | D2 | L2 | SC:N
Q: The ward changes hands when:
A) The shift clock strikes and responsibility transfers with the keys B) The incoming assistant confirms the ward group-chat is fully current C) The outgoing assistant finishes charting and announces they're leaving D) The incoming walks the ward with the sheet, hears the watch-fors, reads back the critical ones
KEY: D. A, B, C are the three ways handoffs happen without information moving.
