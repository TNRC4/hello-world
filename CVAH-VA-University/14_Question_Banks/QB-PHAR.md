### PHAR-001 | PHA-M1 | D2 | Recall | SC:N
Q: Generic vs brand matters at the shelf because:
A) Price only B) The same molecule appears under multiple names; verify by generic name + concentration C) Brands are stronger D) It doesn't
KEY: B.

### PHAR-002 | PHA-M2 | D2 | Apply | SC:Y

### PHAR-003 | PHA-M3 | D2 | Apply | SC:N
