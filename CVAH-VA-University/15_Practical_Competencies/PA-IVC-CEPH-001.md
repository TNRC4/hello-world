# PA-IVC-CEPH-001 — IV Catheter Placement Practical Assessment

**Candidate instructions:** "Place and secure a cephalic IV catheter per this order. Narrate as on shift."
**Evaluator setup:** patient appropriate to stage (model permitted for mechanics re-tests; live placement required for final sign-off), order includes gauge + purpose, holder provided; bench includes multiple gauges (selection scored), one catheter with deliberately damaged packaging (should be rejected).

| # | Observable behavior | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1★ | Order + two-identifier patient check; questions order if mismatched to patient (scripted 18g/kitten probe available) | 2/1/0 |
| 2 | Supplies complete before start: T-port primed, tape pre-torn in order, flush ready; rejects compromised packaging | 2/1/0 |
| 3 | Generous clip; aseptic prep per protocol; no post-prep touch of entry point | 2/1/0 |
| 4 | Distal-first site choice; correct insertion angle; flash recognized | 2/1/0 |
| 5★ | Advances unit 1–2 mm flat BEFORE threading; threads smoothly without force | 2/1/0 |
| 6★ | Never re-advances catheter over stylet; any withdrawal is as a unit (scripted probe if not naturally tested: "it won't thread — show me your options") | 2/1/0 |
| 7★ | Stylet directly to sharps at withdrawal | 2/1/0 |
| 8 | Attaches primed T-port; flush assessment verbalized (resistance? bleb? patient response?) | 2/1/0 |
| 9 | Tape architecture: order, tension (one-finger test demonstrated), stress loop | 2/1/0 |
| 10 | Label complete (date/time/gauge/initials); post-wrap flush recheck | 2/1/0 |
| 11 | Documentation complete; case log entry | 2/1/0 |
| 12★ | Attempt discipline + patient monitoring throughout | 2/1/0 |

**Pass:** ≥19/24, no ★ at 0, zero CFs.
**Critical fails:** re-advance over stylet · stylet left out · flushing against firm resistance · no ID check · exceeding attempt limit · circumferential tight tape left unchecked · fails scripted infiltration recognition ("flush raises a soft bleb — what is that?").
**Remediation defaults:** threading mechanics → model lab ≥5 + video review; securing → tape drills; recognition → W4-03 re-take + INT-06 scenarios.
