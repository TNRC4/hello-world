# PA-RES-001 — Handling & Restraint Practical Assessment (Sections A–D)

**Setup:** two live patients (calm dog, tolerant cat) + evaluator role-plays for escalation probes. Sections may be assessed across separate sessions; all four required for TS-RES sign-offs.

**A. Approach & reading (TS-HND-001):** greets/reads before touch (2/1/0) · oblique low approach, no looming (2/1/0) · ★names patient's current signals when asked (2/1/0) · escape audit before barrier removal (2/1/0).
**B. Lateral restraint (TS-RES-001):** controlled takedown, limbs above joints (2/1/0) · ★neck arm placement clear of trachea (2/1/0) · maintains hold with calm patient monitoring (2/1/0) · controlled release (2/1/0).
**C. Sternal/standing (TS-RES-002):** correct hug-hold geometry, face out of bite arc (2/1/0) · prevents sit/whip proactively (2/1/0) · feline meatloaf + towel option used appropriately (2/1/0).
**D. Venipuncture holds (TS-RES-003):** cephalic occlusion/roll/extension correct (2/1/0) · ★jugular head position without hyperextension + airway watch verbalized (2/1/0) · saphenous occlusion + limb management (2/1/0) · releases occlusion on cue while maintaining restraint (2/1/0).
**Universal ★ probe (scripted):** evaluator narrates "the cat begins open-mouth breathing" mid-hold — candidate must stop/release position and escalate.

**Pass:** ≥80% per section, no ★ at 0, zero CFs. **Critical fails:** tracheal pressure · dropping patient · face-in-bite-arc after correction · continuing through the scripted distress probe · leaving patient unsecured on table.
