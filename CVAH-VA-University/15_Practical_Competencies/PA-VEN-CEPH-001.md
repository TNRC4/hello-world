# PA-VEN-CEPH-001 — Venipuncture Practical Assessment (Cephalic; anchor sets for saphenous/jugular noted)

**Candidate instructions (read aloud):** "Collect the ordered blood sample from this patient with your holder. Narrate anything you'd normally verbalize. Standard stop rules apply. You may ask for anything you'd ask for on shift."
**Evaluator setup:** live patient appropriate to learner stage (or model for re-tests of mechanics), real order (test + volume), holder provided, full supply bench including deliberate inclusion of one WRONG tube type (selection is scored). Prereqs verified: state ≥7, reps logged per spec §24.

| # | Observable behavior | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1★ | Verifies order AND patient identity aloud (two identifiers) before setup | 2/1/0 |
| 2 | Assembles all supplies before positioning; selects correct needle/syringe/tubes for the order | 2/1/0 |
| 3 | Directs holder to correct position + occlusion; confirms holder readiness | 2/1/0 |
| 4 | Palpates and identifies vessel (verbalizes direction/depth); preps site | 2/1/0 |
| 5 | Stabilizes with distal traction; enters bevel-up at appropriate angle | 2/1/0 |
| 6 | Responds correctly to flash (flattens, seats); aspirates gently without collapse | 2/1/0 |
| 7★ | Correct completion order: occlusion released → withdraw → immediate pressure | 2/1/0 |
| 8★ | Sharps directly to container; no recap | 2/1/0 |
| 9 | Transfers promptly, correct fill order, correct mixing (inversions, no shaking) | 2/1/0 |
| 10★ | Labels at patient-side | 2/1/0 |
| 11 | Rechecks site before patient release; notes patient tolerance | 2/1/0 |
| 12 | Documents completely (site, gauge, attempts, complications, initials) | 2/1/0 |
| 13★ | Throughout: monitors patient stress; verbalizes status; would stop appropriately (probe with scripted question if no natural event: "your second attempt just failed — what now?") | 2/1/0 |

**Pass:** ≥21/26 AND no ★ step at 0 AND zero CFs.
**Critical fails:** no ID check · recap/unsafe sharp · third attempt or continuing past stop criteria · ignores overt patient distress (evaluator terminates) · leaves unlabeled sample · unsafe direction to holder (e.g., airway compromise).
**Site-variant anchors:** saphenous — add "manages limb stabilization/rolling vessel" at step 5; jugular — add "neck neutral recheck before release" to step 11 and "verbalizes coagulopathy screening question" to step 1 (★).
**Remediation defaults on fail:** steps 4–6 → model lab + 3 coached draws; step 7/hematoma → mechanics review + coached; step 13 → scenario series SIM-CORE-003. Re-attempt when trainer flags ready, minimum 3 practice days.
