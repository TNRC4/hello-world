# VA3 Workflow-Ownership Attestation (structured form)

Completed independently by two supervising CVTs/DVMs who have worked ≥10 shifts with the candidate in the last 90 days. Each item anchored 1–4 (1 = not yet observed · 2 = with prompting · 3 = reliably · 4 = reliably + teaches others). VA3 requires average ≥3.0 from both raters with no item at 1.

1. Anticipates procedural needs before being asked (setup appears when it should).
2. Self-assigns appropriately from the board without over- or under-reaching scope.
3. Escalates at the right threshold — neither alarm-fatigues the team nor sits on findings.
4. Manages task stack under load; renegotiates priorities out loud.
5. Patient-ID and verification discipline never observed to slip, including when busy.
6. Documentation is real-time, complete, and audit-clean.
7. Case-log honesty: records failures/complications as readily as successes.
8. Peer effect: juniors are safer and calmer when this person is on the floor.

Free-text (required): one incident that best shows this candidate's judgment. | Signature/date per rater.
