# Repetition Requirements (Educational Requirements — Not Legal Rules)

Rationale principle: repetition counts are set where motor-learning and variation coverage justify them, reviewed annually against analytics (`22_Analytics/` attempts-to-competency data), and never inflated for ritual. "Success" = completed to spec without unresolved complication; spread requirements force variation, the real driver of reliability.
