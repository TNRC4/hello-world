# SIM-CORE-001 — The Quietly Deteriorating Ward Patient (unannounced floor sim, Week 7)

**Objective:** detect and correctly escalate a subtle multi-parameter trend while busy with routine load.
**Setup:** sim chart + kennel card for "Moose," 6y MN Rottweiler post-splenectomy Day 1 (model/empty-kennel proxy with planted chart; trainer plays remote "hands" reporting what learner examines). Learner is mid-normal-duties; the sim chart is slipped into their ward round.
**Planted trend:** 08:00 HR 88, gums pink, CRT 1.5s, resp 24, T 100.9, QAR-comfortable · 10:00 HR 104, gums pale-pink, CRT 2s, resp 30, T 100.4, quieter · 12:00 (learner's round) trainer-fed findings: HR 122, gums pale, CRT 2.5s, resp 36 shallow, T 99.8, dull, abdomen "feels fuller."
**Expected actions (scored):** computes the trend (not just the snapshot) · abdomen + gum findings named · SBAR-V to DVM at report-now urgency · does NOT leave to "finish rounds first" · charts report time.
**Debrief:** what single 10:00 finding already justified a mention? (*pale-pink + quieter — trend seeds*) What does post-splenectomy context add? (*internal-bleed pattern-awareness — educational only, no diagnosing*)
**Scoring:** pass = report-now escalation with trend cited; partial = escalated at wrong urgency; fail = charted-and-moved-on (→ remediation: W6-03 + trend case series).
