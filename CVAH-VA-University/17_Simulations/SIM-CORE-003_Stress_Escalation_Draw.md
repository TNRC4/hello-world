# SIM-CORE-003 — The Escalating Cat (roleplay drill, Week 3)

**Objective:** live stop-criteria judgment during venipuncture under social pressure.
**Setup:** trainer plays holder with a realistic cat model (or acts patient-state narration); a second trainer plays a hurried DVM ("I need that sample before my 2 o'clock").
**Script:** attempt 1 fails (vein rolls). Cat narrated: tail lashing, ears back. Attempt 2: flash then collapse; cat begins open-mouth breathing narration mid-aspiration. **Expected:** immediate stop + release position + verbalized escalation — WITH the social pressure line delivered ("seriously? I need it now"). Candidate must hold the stop and propose alternatives (rest + medial saphenous later, CVT takeover, sedation conversation) rather than a third stick.
**Debrief:** where did the pressure land in your body? What sentence works? (Teach the script: "She's open-mouth breathing — I'm stopping. Fastest safe path is X.")
**Scoring:** PA-VEN-CEPH-001 step 13 anchor; fail = third attempt or continuing through distress.
