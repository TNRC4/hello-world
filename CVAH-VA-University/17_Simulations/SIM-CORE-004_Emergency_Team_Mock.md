# SIM-CORE-004 — Emergency Team Mock (Week 7 + quarterly thereafter)

**Objective:** activation, role execution, closed-loop communication under a full team scenario.
**Setup:** CPR manikin/model, crash cart, timer; CVT plays lead; roles drawn from hat (compressor/recorder/runner). Scenario card A: hit-by-car arrival → assessment → arrest at minute 3. Scenario card B: anesthesia-adjacent arrest in recovery. Scenario card C: GDV arrival prep (no arrest — speed-staging drill).
**Scored elements (PA-EMR/ECC anchors):** activation quality (loud/specific/located) · staging time (O2+cart+IV setup <3 min) · compression quality 2-min cycle (rate 100–120 by metronome check, recoil, swap without gap >5 s) · recorder log completeness vs script · closed-loop repeats (3 planted instructions must be repeated back) · role discipline (planted temptation: lead "drops" a drug vial near the runner — drawing/pushing is not theirs).
**Debrief protocol:** timeline walk from recorder's log; one keep/one change per person; restock-and-reseal completed as part of the sim (it's scored).
