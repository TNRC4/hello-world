# Simulation Design Guide

Simulations exist to train Question 3 (recognition) and team choreography where real cases are too rare, too risky, or too fast to learn on. Formats: branching digital scenarios (LMS/H5P), staged floor drills (trainer-rigged findings), tabletop trend cases (paper/LMS vitals tables), and full team mocks (CPR/emergency). Every sim states: objectives, setup, script/branches, expected learner actions, debrief questions, scoring linkage. Digital sims score in LMS; floor drills score via the linked PA anchors.
