# Program Analytics — Specification

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Reviewed quarterly by Manager + Program Administrator; annual repetition-requirement calibration input.

## Learning metrics
time-to-VA1 / VA2 / Core-graduation / VA3 (median + spread, cohort-tracked) · attempts-to-competency per skill (the repetition-requirement calibration signal) · first-time practical pass rate per PA · remediation episodes per learner-domain + pass-after-remediation rate (remediation effectiveness) · quiz/exam item statistics (difficulty, discrimination — retire dead items) · challenge-out usage + downstream performance of challengers (validates the challenge system) · skill-decay events (state-12/14 entries) per skill · specialty completion rates + durations · case-exposure supply vs demand per skill (the scheduling-constraint detector).

## Operational transfer metrics (the design question, measured)
% of routine blood draws performed by signed-off assistants · % of catheter placements (where policy permits) · % of surgical preps · % of scheduled ward rounds · technician minutes returned per week (computed from task counts × standard minutes from the Ownership Matrix) · procedural setup error rate · sample-quality error rate (hemolysis/clot/label incidents per 100 samples) · radiographic retake rate · anesthesia-record completeness rate · patient-safety near-miss reports (COUNT RISES ARE GOOD EARLY — reporting culture indicator; pair with severity trend).

## Guardrails
No metric is ever used to reward speed on safety-critical tasks; individual learner analytics are coaching data (manager/trainer visibility), not leaderboard data; near-miss reporting is never punished via metrics.

## Contribution Index (optional, manager-facing)
Purpose: quantify BREADTH of trained-and-approved workload support — a capacity-planning tool, explicitly not a ranking (never displayed to learners comparatively). Computation: 14 dimensions (handling, exam support, assessment, sample collection, technical procedures, treatment support, inpatient, surgery, anesthesia, dentistry, imaging, emergency, pharmacy, laboratory); each scored 0–4 from passport states of its mapped skills (0 none · 1 any in-training · 2 core skills at 8–9 · 3 core at 10 · 4 specialty credential current); index = sum (0–56) + dimension radar chart. Use: "where does training investment most improve Tuesday's capacity?" — visible as team heatmap in Manager View 3.
