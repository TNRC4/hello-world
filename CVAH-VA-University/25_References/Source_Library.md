# Source Library

Version 1.0 · 2026-08-17 · Rule: every major clinical module cites here; verify currency at deployment and at each annual review. Prioritization per charter: AZ regulatory → federal → specialty organizations → AAHA/AVMA/RECOVER/AAFP → consensus statements → peer-reviewed → major texts → manufacturer docs → pharmacology references.

## Regulatory (Tier 1–2)
- Arizona Revised Statutes Title 32, Ch. 21 (Veterinarians), esp. § 32-2281 (veterinary assistants; supervision). Arizona State Veterinary Medical Examining Board — vetboard.az.gov → Statutes & Rules.
- Arizona Administrative Code Title 3, Ch. 11 (Veterinary Medical Examining Board), esp. R3-11-605 (Certified Veterinary Technician services). azsos.gov compilation; Cornell LII mirror.
- DEA controlled-substance regulations (registrant recordkeeping) — deadiversion.usdoj.gov.
- OSHA: bloodborne pathogens/sharps (29 CFR 1910.1030), hazard communication (1910.1200); NIOSH hazardous-drug list and veterinary safety guidance.
- Arizona radiation control program requirements for X-ray registration/operators — `[verify current agency requirements at deployment]`.

## Clinical guidelines (Tier 3–8)
- RECOVER Initiative 2024 CPR Guidelines — recoverinitiative.org/2024-guidelines; Burkitt-Creedon et al., J Vet Emerg Crit Care 2024 (BLS and ALS guideline papers).
- ACVAA Small Animal Monitoring Guidelines — acvaa.org.
- AAHA guidelines suite (aaha.org): Anesthesia & Monitoring; Fluid Therapy; Infection Control, Prevention & Biosecurity; Technician Utilization; Pain Management; Dental Care.
- AAFP/ISFM feline handling & environment guidelines; current AAHA/AAFP vaccination guidelines (site conventions).
- AVMA policies/resources: medical records, veterinary technology scope resources.
- Perioperative mortality literature: Brodbelt et al. CEPSAF study lineage (recovery-phase risk).

## Texts (Tier 10)
- McCurnin's Clinical Textbook for Veterinary Technicians and Nurses, current ed. (Bassert) — core skills reference.
- Lumb & Jones' Veterinary Anesthesia and Analgesia, current ed. — anesthesia academy backbone.
- Cowell & Tyler's Diagnostic Cytology and Hematology, current ed. — lab bench.
- Thrall, Textbook of Veterinary Diagnostic Radiology + a positioning atlas (Ayers or equivalent), current eds.
- Wiggs & Lobprise / Niemiec veterinary dentistry references, current eds.
- Silverstein & Hopper, Small Animal Critical Care Medicine, current ed. — ECC academy.
- Sophia Yin, Low Stress Handling, Restraint and Behavior Modification — handling curriculum.

## Pharmacology (Tier 12)
- Plumb's Veterinary Drug Handbook / Plumb's Veterinary Drugs (current legally accessed edition/subscription) — paraphrase-only rule per §48; ISMP medication-safety resources (adapted).
